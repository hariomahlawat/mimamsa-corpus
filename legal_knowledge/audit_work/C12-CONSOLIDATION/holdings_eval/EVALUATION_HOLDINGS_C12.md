## Manual adjudication (`ADJUDICATION_SAMPLE.csv`)

Method:
- **Sample.** Drawn with seed `C12-HOLD-EVAL-2026`. The adjudication sheet held no reference-match information, and every label was assigned before the join.
- **Source text.** Each item was read against the page text rendered by PyMuPDF from the PDF git blob, not the extractor's text layer. The surrounding page was read too, to decide voice and role.
- **Labelling rule.** CORRECT_HOLDING means the court's own determination of an issue in the case: a proposition of law, or its decisive application. The following were not counted as correct:
  - operative disposal lines;
  - factual recitals;
  - restatements of a rule;
  - intermediate steps in the reasoning.

## Failure categories (ranked by impact)

1. **Reasoning, recitals and rule restatements promoted to `holding`.** This is 20/60 (33%) of the sample. Examples:
   - #35: "The Medical Board's opinion was clearly to the effect that ..." (a factual premise; the holding is the next sentence);
   - #31: a restatement of Rule 51 of the Army Rules;
   - #54: "In the result, the petition fails and is dismissed."
2. **Multi-opinion attribution.** Split verdicts are not detected, and dissent detection misses cues at the end of an opinion. This is 9/60 (15%) of the sample, all in 2 of 30 judgments, but those judgments are large. Recall for reference holdings in non-sole opinions is 2/12.
3. **Questions and holdings from cited precedents** ("In X, the issue/question before this Court was ...", "The Court came to the conclusion ..."). This is 6/26 of QoL items and 3/60 of holdings.
4. **Low holding recall caused by the per-opinion cap and ranking.** Only about 29% of reference holdings are recovered as `holding`, against about 70% by any court-voice type. Taking only the top-ranked items does not buy precision: rank ≤ 3 gives a reference-match lower bound of 23/90.
5. **Span defects** (3/60): truncation at a page break (#37, #44), or a bare rejection with no content (#18, "We find no merit in this contention.").
6. **Paragraph anchoring** in judgments with unnumbered paragraphs (see Anchoring above).

## What the extractor does NOT cover (directive list), so no credit is given

- **Treatment.** The extractor only copies C11 `TREATMENT_CANDIDATES.csv` rows when page and text overlap. On these 30 judgments, 0 items carry a treatment row. No overruling or other treatment is inferred, and this evaluation infers none either.
- **Statute links.** These are C11 `STATUTE_JUDGMENT_LINKS.csv` rows copied unchanged (128 items), plus surface-level statute references normalised from the sentence (489 items). Neither was evaluated for correctness, and no credit is given.
- **Versions** (point-in-time validity of the provisions a holding applies) and **scenarios** (applying holdings to fact patterns) are not produced at all.
- **Ratio determination.** Every item stays `AUTOMATED_CANDIDATE_NOT_VERIFIED`. `holding` means only a candidate holding sentence.

## Limitations

- **Single adjudicator, and an AI.** All 86 adjudication labels were assigned by one AI evaluator, with no second rater and no agreement statistics. The boundary between CORRECT_HOLDING and REASONING_NOT_HOLDING is a judgment call. A more lenient rater who counted operative disposals and premises as holdings would push true precision up by roughly 5 to 10 points.
- **The reference set is one blind annotator's work.** It is not exhaustive: about 4 holdings per judgment, and QoL excerpts are often anchor sentences, not questions. Recall is measured against that annotator's choices.
- **Not legal validation.** No lawyer reviewed the labels or the reference set.
- **Small sample with clustering.** 60 holdings and 26 QoL items. The Wilson CIs are pooled; see the macro figures.
- **Two adjudicated judgments are atypical.** Gene Campaign is a GM-crop environmental case, not military law, though it is classed DIRECT_MILITARY_LAW upstream; the reference annotator notes the same.
- **Pre-registration deviation.** The post-hoc paragraph locator is reported alongside the pre-registered strict paragraph check and is labelled as post-hoc. No other metric, threshold or label was changed after results were seen.
