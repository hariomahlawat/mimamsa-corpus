## Reading (research only)
- The 1961 instrument performs **one substitution of the whole phrase**. MML 2 splits that phrase into two bracketed parts with two attributions.
- "SRO 1, dated 12th March, 1964" pairs the **number** of S.R.O. 1/1961 with the **date** of S.R.O. 126/1964 (12-03-1964).
- A conflation of the two instruments is the most economical reading. It is **not** established.
