## Questions by priority
1. **LAQ-02: Army Rule 182 (court-of-inquiry evidence).** S.R.O. 17(E)/1993 item 36, as printed (Gazette 06-12-1993 p.18; file e9bf2114…), reads "shall not be admissible in nor shall any evidence…". The words "evidence against a person subject to the Act," are missing. They are present in the 1954 text (p.341), the India Code compilation and MML 2. Four readings agree on the omission (SC4-01): Reviewers A and C, the coordinator and the technical reviewer.
   - Is the substituted rule to be read with those words supplied, as a printing error?
   - Is a corrigendum required, or held, before relying on the exclusion?

   Related point: COI-003#P6 says not to treat *all* court-of-inquiry statements as admissible, which implies that some are admissible against the person. The reviewers read r.182, in both its 1954 and 1993 forms, as excluding them except at a trial for giving false evidence, with the 1993 proviso allowing use for cross-examination. Whether that reading holds given the printed omission is the question asked above, and no determination is made here. Severities: D P1 (raised from P2 after coordinator QC-03), A P2, C P2, B P3.
