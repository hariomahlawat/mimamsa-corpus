## B. Draft request (for the owner to approve, adapt and send through an authorised channel)

**To:** (i) Controller of Publications, Department of Publication, Government of India; (ii) Central Secretariat Library (Gazette collection); (iii) National Archives of India (Public Records); copy (iv) Ministry of Defence, Department of Military Affairs / JAG Branch records, where appropriate.

**Subject:** Request for an authenticated copy of the Gazette of India, Part II, Section 4, No. 47, dated 27 November 1954 (S.R.O. 484 — Army Rules, 1954), and of later amendments to Rule 23.

1. Please provide a certified or custodian-authenticated scan of the **Gazette of India, Part II, Section 4, No. 47, 27 November 1954**, pp. **291–378**, containing Ministry of Defence notification **S.R.O. 484** (the Army Rules, 1954), including the masthead, issue/part/section, the notification header and signature block.
2. At minimum, please include **Rules 22, 23, 180 and 182**, and any **errata or corrigenda** published for S.R.O. 484.
3. Please also provide authenticated copies of any notification amending **Rule 23** after 1954. The following amending notifications are cited in compilations, and copies of those affecting Rules 22–25, 180 and 182 are requested together with their commencement dates: S.R.O. 34 (19-01-1963); S.R.O. 116 (1965); S.R.O. 188 (04-06-1979); S.R.O. 216 (30-08-1988); S.R.O. 17(E) (06-12-1993); S.R.O. 258 (27-11-2002); S.R.O. 17(E) (28-12-2019).
4. If the material is held only in bound volumes, please state the archive reference, volume and page numbers.
5. The copies are required solely to establish the statutory provenance of a private legal research database. No part will be published without legal scrutiny.

## C. Evidence requirements before the dispute can be closed (acceptance gate)

1. An image-based copy (not re-typed) of the 1954 Gazette pages carrying Rule 23(3), with custodian identity, archive reference, date received, file SHA-256 and the name of the person who verified it.
2. The complete amendment chain for Rule 23, from 1954 to the present (every amending S.R.O., or a custodian's statement that none exists), each with a commencement date.
3. An independent second reading of the image text by the independent-reviewer, with the negation checked against the image itself, not OCR.
4. A legal-advisor decision on the operative wording at each relevant date, and on how the database should flag records quoting either wording (HCE-010, EV-016, KB-006, V9-CL-04, V9-SC-19, HCE-008 and any others found).
5. Until gates 1–4 are met, every finding on Rule 23(3) stays **CONFLICTING / BLOCKED**, and no record wording is changed.

## D. What happens if the original cannot be obtained
The finding remains CONFLICTING and the legal advisor decides an interim database annotation. The disputed wording is never "corrected" from a manual or a compilation.
