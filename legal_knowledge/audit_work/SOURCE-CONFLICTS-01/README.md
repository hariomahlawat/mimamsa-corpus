# Source-conflict determinations (owner rule, 2026-11-22)

`conflict_determinations.csv` applies `docs/legal-audit/SOURCE_AUTHORITY_POLICY.md (v1)` to every known conflict. For each document it records:
- authority
- provenance
- applicable date
- amendment history

From those it gives a **research determination of evidentiary weight**. **This is not legal approval.** Statuses stay CONFLICTING or OPEN until legal review. No original record, finding or advisor-packet item was changed.
