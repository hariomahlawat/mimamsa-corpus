## What was checked
- **V1 hashes:** sha256, md5, sha1 and byte counts were recomputed for all 41 held paths (34 proposals). Every value equals both the register and held_copy_facts.csv.
- **V1 evidence references:** every cited evidence reference (file#row) was opened by script, 176 lines of output. Results:
  - **L1:** official_sha256 equals the held bytes.
  - **L2:** the ACQ-02 snapshot matches size, MD5 and SHA-1. The ACQ-01 snapshot matches **MD5 and size only (it has no SHA-1)**. See F-01.
  - **L3 and L4:** the record's sha256 equals the held bytes. For AP-27 the sha256 appears only inside another record's locator (F-05).
  - **Page-image checks and R23 rows:** they exist and carry the document sha256 and a 64-hex render hash.
  - **Filenames:** no proposal relies on a filename or path alone.
- **V2 identity:**
  - **Gazettes:** 10 Gazette identity pages were rendered (AP-01, 03, 04, 07, 11, 15, 17, 19, 20, 22), plus the RS and ER 2023 pages. S.R.O. number, date, Part/Section and printed page match the titles. The one exception is the AP-22 masthead number (English No.15 / Hindi No.16, F-04).
  - **Court copies:** for all 10 court copies, the parties, case number and date on p.1 and the last page match the titles.
- **V3 classes:**
  - The 21 archive.org Gazettes take PRIMARY_GAZETTE_ARCHIVAL_FACSIMILE, which matches Cycle 4.
  - The court copies take PRIMARY_OFFICIAL_COURT_COPY, which matches Cycle 4. AP-28's carrier is recorded as UNKNOWN, which is inconsistent with that class (F-06).
  - The Rajya Sabha record and the incomplete ER 2023 copy are correctly non-primary.
  - Act 13/1975 is **not** left identity-only. It is promoted on weaker evidence than the Act 37/1992 precedent (F-03).
- **V4 contradictions:**
  - No committed record pairs a proposed URL with a different sha256, and no dispute is recorded against any proposed URL.
  - The commit is additive only.
  - Isaac is absent from the proposals and remains AUTHENTICATION_PENDING (F-12).
- **V5 Rajbir:** the three copies are the same judgment. Each copy has its own sha256 evidence, and no status is transferred between them. C2's evidence is the weakest (F-05, F-13).
- **V6 reproducibility:**
  - `--check` returns rc 0 and the unit tests pass 12/12.
  - D4 reproduces exactly: copies 12→44, sources 12→42, KB unchanged, cases 6/7/40 → 13/0/40.
  - Exactly the 7 case records named in D1 change (F-14).
- **V7 method weaknesses:** MD5_SIZE is allowed to reach DOCUMENTED (F-02). The other issues are minor:
  - L4 discovery rows are accepted as locators (F-03).
  - A URL noted in another record's locator is accepted (F-05).
