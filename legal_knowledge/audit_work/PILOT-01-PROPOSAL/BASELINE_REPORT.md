**Validator gaps to close before scoring.** These would be added as new code in a separate reviewed PR, not by editing the existing validator silently:
1. Check that `record_id` exists in the 658 and that `original_sha256` equals that record's `line_sha256`.
2. Check that `batch_id` matches the folder and that the record is in `pilot-selection.json`.
3. Check that any INDEPENDENT_AGENT review of a P0/P1 finding exists and is recorded as blind or not blind. The schema has no independence field; propose one.
4. The finding schema has no temporal-applicability or pinpoint field, though EVIDENCE_STANDARD requires them; propose them.
5. Source records cited as SOURCE_VERIFIED must have `authenticity` ≠ SECONDARY.
6. Re-hash both originals at every checkpoint.

**Acceptance (GO/NO-GO) criteria.** The proposed thresholds must be fixed before scoring:
- Hard NO-GO: any invented or non-existent source survives; an original file hash changes; `validate.py --strict` has errors; any P0 is shown as resolved without primary evidence (including r.23(3)); a SOURCE_VERIFIED finding lacks a pinpoint and source SHA; any record or review claims JAG or formal approval.
- 100% of the 25 records reach a terminal verification state, with "not checked" listed explicitly.
- 100% of P0/P1 findings have an independent review and an advisor disposition.
- Proposed thresholds:
  - ≥ 90% of KB citations in the pilot resolved to a source register entry, or marked unresolvable with a reason.
  - ≥ 80% of statutory claims checked against an official text with version and date.
  - False-positive rate on the advisor-adjudicated set ≤ 15%.
  - Both controls show no P0/P1 that the advisor upholds as a false positive.
- Scorecard metrics follow PILOT_AND_ACCEPTANCE.md. No single "legal accuracy %" will be reported.
