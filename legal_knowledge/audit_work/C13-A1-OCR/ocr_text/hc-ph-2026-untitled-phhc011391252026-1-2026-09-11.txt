2. Learned counsel for the applicant-petitioners submits that the
rider which has been put by the Court to the effect that in case the judgment
in Civil Appeal arising out of Diary No.27446 of 2023 titled as Union of
India and others vs. Balakrishnan Mullikote, decided on 24.03.2026 is not
complied with by considering the claim of the respondent, such action will
be viewed strictly even contempt proceedings can be initiated in case
needed.
3. On being asked as to whether or not passing of such direction
is not within the jurisdiction of the Court, learned counsel for the applicant-
petitioners has not been able to dispute that in case an order passed by this
Court is not complied with within the time-bound manner stipulated therein,
the same will invite contempt proceedings, review is permissible qua such
direction given.
4. Review application stands dismissed.
5. Learned counsel for the parties agree that the time period for
complying with the order dated 02.04.2026 be extended by another period
of eight weeks.
6. Ordered accordingly.
