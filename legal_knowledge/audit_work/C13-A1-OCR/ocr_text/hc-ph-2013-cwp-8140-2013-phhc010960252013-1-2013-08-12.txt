Heard learned counsel for the parties.
The petitioner joined the Armed Forces Medical Corps, as a Short
Service Commissioned Officer on 31.03.2008. He was assigned to the Air
Force in the rank of Squadron Leader. The Integrated Headquarter Ministry
of Defence (Air), Directorate General Medical Services (Air) Med-1, Air
Headquarters (respondent No.4) on 02.11.2011 conveyed advisory/adverse
remarks relating to the period of service of the petitioner from 01.06.2010 to
22.05.2011. The petitioner did not file any statutory or non-statutory
complaint against the said remarks for about 15 months. He, however filed
an original application before the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh
Regional Bench, Chandimandir (respondent No.1) on 19.02.2013. The
interim relief to the petitioner has been declined by the learned Armed
Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Regional Bench, Chandimandir (respondent
No.1) in terms of order dated 09.04.2013 (Annexure P-4). The petitioner
thereafter received speaking order (Annexure P-5) on 10.04.2013, wherein
it is inter alia stated that he was graded 'low average' by the First Technical
Officer (FTO) along with some adverse/advisory remarks. The petitioner
failed to represent within 60 days of the receipt of the said communication.
The speaking order (Annexure P-5) inter alia mentions that after
considering the material on record, the Air Officer Commanding in Chief
Western Air Command was of the opinion that the assessment made by the
First Technical Officer was not in synchronisation with the Assistant
Initiating Officer, Review Officer, Senior Review Officer and Senior
Technical Officer. Therefore, the Air Officer Commanding in Chief Western
