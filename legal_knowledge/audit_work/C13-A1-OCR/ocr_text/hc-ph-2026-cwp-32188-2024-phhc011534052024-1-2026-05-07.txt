HARSIMRAN SINGH SETHI J. (ORAL)
1. Present writ petition has been filed challenging the order dated
30.09.2022 (Annexure P-1) passed by respondent No.1- Armed Forces
Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh by which, the special family pension
has been given in favour of respondent No.2.
2. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that in the present
case, the husband of respondent No.2 fell from cycle and succumbed to the
injuries while availing benefit of casual leave hence, there was no causal
connection of death of husband of respondent No.2 with the military service
and therefore, said death cannot be treated as attributable to the military
service hence, the grant of benefit of special family pension in favour of
respondent No.2 by treating the death attributable to the military service is
incorrect and therefore, the order dated 30.09.2022 (Annexure P-1) passed
by the Tribunal is liable to be set aside.

3. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent No.2, on
the other hand, submits that after the death of husband of respondent No.2,
a Court of enquiry was held wherein, after appreciating all the facts, the
Court of enquiry found that the death of husband of respondent No.2 is
attributable to the military service which recommendation of the Court of
enquiry still exists hence, the petitioners cannot be allowed to say that the
death is not attributable to the military service. He further submits that
though husband of respondent No.2 was on casual leave, but same is to be
treated as active military service keeping in view the rules governing the
service.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that though the
Court of enquiry had given such recommendations but the administrative
authority has not accepted the same and therefore, no benefit of such
recommendation can be given in favour of respondent No.2 so as to held her
entitled for grant of benefit of special family pension.
5. We have heard learned counsel for the parties and have gone
through the record with their able assistance.
6. Once, it is a conceded position that after the death of husband
of respondent No.2, a Court of enquiry was held wherein, after considering
all the facts, a finding was recorded that that the death of the husband of
respondent No.2 was attributable to the military service and as such the
same has to be taken to the logical end so as to treat the said death
attributable to the military service and grant the consequential benefits. The
view taken by the Tribunal is perfectly valid and legal.

7. The argument which is being raised by the learned counsel for
the petitioners is that the recommendation of the Court of enquiry were not
accepted by the administrative authorities and therefore, no benefit of the
same could have been given.
8. It may be noticed that once, the competent authority after
appreciating all the facts had made a recommendation, without any valid
justification, the same cannot be set aside by the executive so as to deny the
benefit. Further, recommendation of the administrative authorities cannot
be given precedence over finding of Court of enquiry which is based upon
evidence brought on record. Hence, the grant of benefit of special family
pension on the basis of the recommendation of the Court of enquiry is
perfectly valid and legal. The order dated 30.09.2022 (Annexure P-1)
passed by the Tribunal is not perverse either on the basis of the facts or on
the basis of law.
9. At this stage, learned counsel for the petitioners submits that
the benefit of whole of the arrears has been given whereas, it should have
been restricted as the claim was raised after a period of 18 years.
10. The said argument cannot be accepted in view of the settled
principles of law settled by the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India in Civil
Appeal No.3086 of 2012 titled "Balbir Singh vs. Union of India and others",
decided on 08.04.2016 as well as in Civil Appeal Nos. 6820-6824 of 2018
titled as Union of India through Secretary and others vs. SGT Girish
Kumar and others, decided on 12.02.2026.
11. No ground is made out for any interference by this Court in the
facts and circumstances of the present case.
