The Conspectus Of Facts
2. The petitioner, a retired officer of the Armed Forces, rendered more
than thirty-four years of continuous and unblemished service and was released
from service on attaining the age of superannuation on 01.01.1997. At the
time of retirement, the petitioner was suffering from Stage-I Hypertension,
however, during the Release Medical Examination conducted on 18.09.1996
he was erroneously placed in medical category SHAPE-l and no disability
was recorded.Subsequently,upon review  of medical records and
consultations with specialists in the year 2018, it came to light that the
condition had progressed to Stage-II Hypertension with ischemic attacks and
that the disability had escaped proper assessment at the time of release. The
petitioner thereafter asserted that he had become entitled to consideration for
disability pension under the applicable pensionary rules and the Guide to
Medical Officers (Military Pensions), 2008.
3. The petitioner pursued his claim before the authorities, which was
rejected, compelling him to approach the Armed Forces Tribunal by filing
Original Application No. 1134 of 2023 seeking grant of disability pension.
The Tribunal dismissed the application solely on the ground of delay by
relying upon Union of India v. Ex. Sep. R. Munusamy, Civil Appeal No.
6536 of 2021, reported as 2022 INSC 731 without entering into the merits of
the claim.
