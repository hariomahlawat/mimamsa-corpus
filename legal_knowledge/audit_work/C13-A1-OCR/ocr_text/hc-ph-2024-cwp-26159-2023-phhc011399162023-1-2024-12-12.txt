The present petitioner becomes aggrieved from the impugned order
made upon OA-704 if 2014 on 24.08.2023 (Annexure P-8) by the learned Armed
Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench (in short hereinafter to be referred as the
Tribunal'), wherebys the claim raised thereins by the petitioner for setting aside
of the dismissal order, as became passed on 24th July, 2008, became rejected.
2. The reasons which have been, so made, in the said order became
comprised in the factum, that the said supra OA was completely time barred, and
no application became appended with the OA, wherebys relief was sought for
condonation of the delay which so occurred in the institution of the OA before the
Tribunal.

pass the impugned order became anviled on the factum that the apposite dismissal
order was passed on 24th July 2008, wherein, the applicant challenged the said

to condone the inordinate delay. ""
