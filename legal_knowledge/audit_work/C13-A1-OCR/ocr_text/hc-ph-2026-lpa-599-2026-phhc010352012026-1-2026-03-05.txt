1. Learned Single Judge has dismissed the writ petition filed by the
appellant only on the ground that no part of cause of action has arisen within
the territorial limits of this Court. For coming to such conclusion, the learned
Single Judge has observed that mere inter se communication between different
offices would not constitute any part of cause of action.
2. It is not in dispute that the appellant was granted contractual
employment by the authorities based at Chandimandir Cantt., Panchkula,
Haryana. His services have been brought to an end by the same office. It is
therefore submitted that mere fact that the appellant was working at Nalagarh,
Himachal Pradesh would not be material, since a part of cause of action has

4. Considering the undisputed position that the appointing authority

the learned Single Judge that no part of cause of action has arisen within the
territorial limits of this Court.

as under:-
