ALOK JAIN, J. (Oral)
1. The present petition has been filed seeking issuance of writ in
the nature of mandamus directing respondent Nos.2 and 3 to refund the
wrongfully recovered amount of Rs.73,734/- at the time of final settlement
of retiral dues and it is also prayed that the legal notice dated 21.01.2019 and
reminder dated 24.05.2019 (Annexure P-1 and P-2) be also decided.
2. Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the
amount retracted from the date of retirement and thereafter, the petitioner is
made to run from pillar to post.
3. Learned counsel for the respondents has filed his response and
has taken preliminary objection that the matter is squarely covered under the
provisions of Section 3(o) of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 and
hence, the present petition would not be maintainable as per Section 14 of
the AFT Act, 2007, due to lack of jurisdiction.
4. Faced with the above, learned counsel for the petitioner submits
that the petition was filed in the year 2o19 and thereafter, the respondents
