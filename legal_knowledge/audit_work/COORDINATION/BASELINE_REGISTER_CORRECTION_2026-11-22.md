**Modelling (revised after independent review).** `effective_date` always holds the stated commencement, consistent with every "in force on publication" row (e.g. AR-028). A retrospective deemed date has its own machine-readable column, `retrospective_deemed_effect_from`, in the register and chronology. `check_registers.py` now enforces three rules for it: an ISO date; only on a row with a STATED commencement; earlier than that commencement. The rules are tested in `tests/test_legal_audit_retrospective_deemed.py`; 4 of its 6 tests failed before the rule was added. Publication date (1988-09-17), stated commencement (1988-09-17) and deemed effect (1979-06-04) remain three distinct fields.

**Observations, not changed here.** The short titles are printed as "Army (1st Amendment) Rules, 1965" (S.R.O. 116, PIC-37) and "Army (2nd Amendment) Rules, 1965" (S.R.O. 215, PIC-38), while the register's `short_title_as_printed` reads "First" and "Second". Both are left for a later as-printed fix.

**Page coverage.** PIC-37 covers p.96, which carries r.1, the only place a commencement clause would appear. The continuation page p.97 (r.2(b), App. I Form I) was image-checked earlier (`continuation_pages_status` ALL_IMAGE_CHECKED).
