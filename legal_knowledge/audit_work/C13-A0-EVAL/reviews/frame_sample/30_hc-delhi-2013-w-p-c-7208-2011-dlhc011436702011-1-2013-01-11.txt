GITA MITTAL, J.

1.  Thomas Jefferson said that “Experience has shown, that

even under the best forms of government those entrusted with

power have, in time, and by slow operations, perverted it into

tyranny”.

Tyranny points towards arbitrariness.  The instant case

raises this issue with full force.

2.

September 2011 passed by the Armed Forces Tribunal rejecting

O.A.No.161/2011.  By this petition, the petitioner had

challenged the legality of the action of the respondents in
