Neutral Citation No. - 2023:AHC:227047

Court No. - 34

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 19331 of 2023

Petitioner :- Rambir Rathor
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Rama Nand Shukla,Mani Shanker Pandey
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Anil Kumar Rai

Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

By means of this petition filed under Article 226 of the
Constitution, petitioner has prayed for certain directions to the
authorities for grant of full pension including other pensionary
benefits.

Learned counsel for the respondents Sri Anil Kumar Rai has raised
preliminary objection regarding maintainability of this petition
because the department in question stands notified under the
Central Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 and submits that the
petitioner is an ex Army personnel and as such can avail such
remedy before the Tribunal.

I view of the above, I decline to interfere in the matter. However,
liberty rests with the petitioner to apply before the Armed Forces
Tribunal, if so advised.

Subject to aforesaid liberty, this petition consigned to records.

Order Date :- 1.12.2023
IrfanUddin

Digitally signed by :-
IRFAN UDDIN SIDDIKI
High Court of Judicature at Allahabad
