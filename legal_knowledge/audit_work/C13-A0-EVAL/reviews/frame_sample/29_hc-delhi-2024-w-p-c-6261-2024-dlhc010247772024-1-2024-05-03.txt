Tribunal challenging the order dated March 14, 2024.

3.
Learned counsel for the petitioner would submit that the reason for

filing the writ petition in this Court is because the petitioner is seeking

interim protection qua the respondent No.4 which is not amenable to the

jurisdiction of the Armed Forces Tribunal. We say nothing on the

submission.

4.
Appropriate shall be for the petitioner to approach the Armed Forces

Tribunal with an interim prayer qua respondent No.4 and it goes without

saying that the Armed Forces Tribunal shall consider the prayer for interim

relief in accordance with law. The writ petition is disposed of.
