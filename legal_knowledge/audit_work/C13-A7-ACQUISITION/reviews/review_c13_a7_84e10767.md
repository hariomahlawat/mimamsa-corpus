## 4. egazette TLS

**Independent reproduction** (openssl 3.0.13 through the agent proxy; the proxy passes this host's real certificate through):
- The server sends **1 certificate**: the leaf `CN=egazette.gov.in`.
  - Issuer: Let's Encrypt YR1.
  - Valid 2026-10-16 to 2027-01-14.
  - SHA-256 fingerprint `64:31:E5:84:…:BC:30:67`, byte-identical to A7's saved leaf.
  - AIA: `http://yr1.i.lencr.org/`.
- I fetched the AIA certificates myself:

| Certificate | Size | DER SHA-256 | Validity | Constraints |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `yr1.i.lencr.org` → YR1 (issuer Root YR) | 1,247 B | `13949634…5758d3f` | 2025-09-03 to 2028-09-02 | CA:TRUE pathlen:0, EKU serverAuth |
| `yr.i.lencr.org` → Root YR (issuer ISRG Root X1) | 1,528 B | `072639d0…5c07cf76` | 2026-05-13 to 2032-09-02 | CA:TRUE, EKU serverAuth |

- Both certificates equal the pins, A7's DER files and the patch PEM.
- `openssl verify -x509_strict -purpose sslserver -verify_hostname egazette.gov.in` gives **OK**. The chain runs leaf → YR1 → Root YR → **ISRG Root X1**, self-signed, in the bundle; SHA-256 `96:BC:EC:06:…:08:C6`, the well-known value.
- Each of these fails verification:
  - the wrong hostname;
  - YR1 only;
  - the leaf only.

**Python 3.13 live checks** against robots.txt, 4 requests at least 3 s apart. Python 3.13 sets `VERIFY_X509_PARTIAL_CHAIN` by default.

| Configuration | Result |
|---|---|
| Bundle only | FAIL |
| YR1 only, partial chain cleared | FAIL |
| **YR1 only, partial chain left at default** | **404, i.e. succeeds**: the intermediate becomes a trust anchor |
| YR1 + Root YR, partial chain cleared (A7's and 0003's configuration) | 404, OK |

Clearing the flag is therefore load-bearing. Both A7's `a7net.egazette_context` and 0003's `tls_verify_for` clear it, keep `CERT_REQUIRED` and `check_hostname=True`, and refuse a PEM whose certificates differ from the pins.

**Assessment**
- **Verification was not disabled or weakened** in the acquisition run. The chain still validates to a trusted root, with full hostname and expiry checks.
- **But the trust configuration was applied without acceptance.** It was used for 47 egazette requests and 17 preserved files before D-04. → **F04: P2 process deviation, no security impact.** It was disclosed by A7.
- **Remedy**: accept the mechanism retroactively, or re-fetch the 17 files under accepted code. Their sha256 pins make either path checkable.
- Minor:
  - One diagnostic request in `MANUAL_TLS_REQUESTS.csv` (10:07:51Z) ran with partial-chain trust **on**, the weakened mode. It fetched robots.txt only.
  - The egazette connections at 10:07:51 and 10:07:52 were about 1 s apart; the second failed at the TLS stage.
  - → F05 (P3).
