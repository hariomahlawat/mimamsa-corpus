## Union of India v Angad Singh Titaria

**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-2015-L03`  
**Decision:** 24 February 2015  
**Documentary source:** `data/raw/sci/2015/77919f00c20215f08db90327b55431559a291f171d001dcdfe789d04b150daf1.pdf`

**Review locators:** 1 and 15–16; full 16-page SCR copy

### Material facts / procedural posture
An Indian Air Force disability-pension matter involving a Medical Board's unexplained NANA categorisation.

### Questions before the Court
What weight an unreasoned medical attribution determination deserves and whether pension could be denied on the facts under the Air Force regulatory scheme.

### Historical statutory framework
Indian Air Force Pension Regulations 1961, Regulation 153; historical Entitlement Rules as applicable

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
The Supreme Court rejected the Union's challenge in the case. Treat it as an AIR FORCE authority and cross-service comparator, not as direct construction of an Army-specific regulation.

### Final disposition and relief
Union appeal dismissed, subject to exact source-page verification of orders and relief.

### Temporal and factual limits
A source discrepancy is open: the 2015 original says IAF Pension Regulation 153, whereas the 2026 N.C. Isaac judgment page 61 describes the reference as an Army regulation. Retain both texts without silent correction.

### Subsequent precedent treatment
2026 INSC 993 page 61 discusses the case; the phrase 'continue to hold the field' appearing at page 69 paragraph 19.4 is a RESPONDENTS' submission, not itself the Court's holding.
