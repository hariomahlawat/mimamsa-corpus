## Controller of Defence Accounts (Pension) v S. Balachandran Nair

**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-2005-L08`  
**Decision:** 21 October 2005  
**Documentary source:** `data/raw/sci/2005/f696a5541cd41d4712aeb94c86d993f364f76e37de7008507cc1c8597548716a.pdf`  
**Source tier:** `PUBLISHED_SCR_COPY_SHA_VERIFIED_JUDIS_BYTE_PARITY_UNKNOWN`  
**Review locators:** 8 (last page of 8-page SCR copy)

### Material facts / procedural posture
An armed-forces pension dispute involved a medical assessment finding disability neither attributable to nor aggravated by service.

### Questions before the Court
Whether the medical record justified disability pension under the applicable historical regulations.

### Historical statutory framework
Historical Army disability-pension scheme, Medical Board causation assessment

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
The Court allowed the Union's appeal on the evidence and regulatory framework before it. The case cannot support a blanket conclusion that every Medical Board opinion is decisive irrespective of the adequacy of reasons.

### Final disposition and relief
Government appeal allowed; no recovery of amounts already paid to the claimant.

### Temporal and factual limits
Contemporaneous Board evidence, rule versions and later Supreme Court emphasis on reasoned medical assessments are essential qualifications.

### Subsequent precedent treatment
Expressly discussed in 2026 INSC 993, PDF pages 49–50, in its survey of pre-Dharamvir case law.

### Independent JAG review questions

2. Distinguish no-recovery relief from the merits of entitlement.
