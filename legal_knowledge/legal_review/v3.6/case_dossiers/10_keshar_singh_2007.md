**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-2007-L10`  
**Decision:** 20 April 2007  
**Documentary source:** `data/raw/sci/2007/89eb15de4987dc7b46c2acf1c2c448d38528e066b3d2479146b2154f3df8676a.pdf`

**Review locators:** 7–8 (closing opinion)

### Material facts / procedural posture
The Union disputed attribution/aggravation of disability to military service in the claimant's medical record.

### Questions before the Court
Whether disability-pension entitlement followed from that service and medical evidence.

### Historical statutory framework
Army pension regulations and entitlement rules operative in the historical disability assessment

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
The Court allowed the Government appeal on the medical record and statutory conditions in that case. Do not extrapolate a universal rule against NANA pension claims.

### Final disposition and relief
Union appeal allowed; sums previously paid protected against recovery.

### Temporal and factual limits
The older regulatory scheme and later presumption jurisprudence can materially change relevance to different discharge dates.
