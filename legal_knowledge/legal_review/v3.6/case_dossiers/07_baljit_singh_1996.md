## Union of India v Baljit Singh

**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-1996-L07`  
**Decision:** 11 October 1996  
**Documentary source:** `data/raw/sci/1996/05e9484593272fe057eef0c133d815b97998c42d12e09c4e012aab1dfbb86646.pdf`  
**Source tier:** `PUBLISHED_SCR_COPY_SHA_VERIFIED_JUDIS_BYTE_PARITY_UNKNOWN`  
**Review locators:** 2–3 (3-page SCR source)

### Material facts / procedural posture
The Union contested a grant of disability pension under the then-existing attribution/aggravation framework and assessment of the claimant's medical record.

### Questions before the Court
Whether pension entitlement was established on the medical facts and what effect should be given to the reasoning used by the High Court.

### Historical statutory framework
Historical Army Pension Regulations 1961, Regulation 173 and then-operative entitlement rules

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
The Court criticised part of the lower-court reasoning but ultimately dismissed the Union's appeal on the case's facts. It is a serious error to index this as 'Government appeal allowed' simply because part of the High Court's reasoning was not endorsed.

### Final disposition and relief
Union of India appeal dismissed (source PDF closing page 3). The archived text contains a 'Baltit' party-name spelling variant but agrees on the case number 13272/1996 and decision date.

### Temporal and factual limits
Older medical-board/causation framework; source transcription may contain errors; no blanket current-law instruction follows from the outcome.

### Subsequent precedent treatment
Directly cited in 2026 INSC 993 in historical analysis on pages 50–51; citation is not a finding that the entire earlier judgment was affirmed or overruled.
