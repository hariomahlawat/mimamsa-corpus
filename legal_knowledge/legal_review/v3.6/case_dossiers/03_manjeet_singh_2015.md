## Union of India v Manjeet Singh

**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-2015-L02`  
**Decision:** 12 May 2015  
**Documentary source:** `data/raw/sci/2015/c2f51fac25017d104cf5040bfc4eeac88b9111f5ea1f514eddb11d81465741dc.pdf`  
**Source tier:** `PUBLISHED_SCR_COPY_SHA_VERIFIED_JUDIS_BYTE_PARITY_UNKNOWN`  
**Review locators:** 24–27 (final reasoning and disposition); full 27-page SCR copy

### Material facts / procedural posture
A disability-pension dispute arising from medical invalidment and a Medical Board finding that disability was neither attributable to nor aggravated by military service, with the reasons underlying that assessment in issue.

### Questions before the Court
Whether a summary or unsupported NANA Medical Board conclusion could justify denial of the disability element in the case's regulatory and evidentiary context.

### Historical statutory framework
Army Pension Regulations applicable to the claimant; historical disability-pension attribution/aggravation framework, Entitlement Rules 1982/2008 applicability to be established

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
The Court held against the Union in the particular record, in which the Medical Board's attribution finding lacked sufficient explanation. The principle should be confined to the medical evidence and rule regime actually considered.

### Final disposition and relief
Union appeals dismissed; source full-opinion closing pages must be used for exact relief.

### Temporal and factual limits
Do not translate a case-specific rejection of an unreasoned Board opinion into a rule that every NANA conclusion is invalid. 2026 INSC 993 requires due weight to proper medical reasons.

### Subsequent precedent treatment
Explicitly discussed in 2026 INSC 993, PDF pages 60–61, but the precise relationship must be evaluated from the Court's reasoning, not a blanket case-wide status.
