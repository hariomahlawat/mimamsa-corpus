## Union of India v Surinder Singh Rathore

**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-2008-L09`  
**Decision:** 13 March 2008  
**Documentary source:** `data/raw/sci/2008/c295f22913ef898ebc2c8815e2fb1c9c645d462a05619b9701da8a8ca1fd7bdf.pdf`

### Material facts / procedural posture
The Union challenged disability pension where the Board found that the relevant medical condition was not attributable to or aggravated by service.

### Questions before the Court
How the contemporaneous medical record and medical-board assessment affected entitlement on the case's facts.

### Historical statutory framework
Historical Army Pension Regulations 1961 Regulation 173, Medical Board attribution/aggravation

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
The Court accepted the Government challenge on that record; it discussed Keshar Singh, creating a direct 2007→2008 citation and an indirect route to the 2026 Isaac decision.

### Final disposition and relief
Government appeal allowed, disability-pension award set aside; sums already paid not to be recovered.

### Temporal and factual limits
Do not equate this with absolute medical-board finality. Adequacy of reasons and subsequent Supreme Court authority require individual analysis.

### Subsequent precedent treatment
