## Union of India v S.R. Dhingra

**Curated ID:** `DCLR-SC-2008-L05`  
**Decision:** 14 December 2007  
**Documentary source:** Original court PDF not archived  
**Source tier:** `SECONDARY_TRANSCRIPT_ONLY_ORIGINAL_COURT_PDF_NOT_ARCHIVED`  
**Review locators:** No PDF source in canonical archive; transcript paras 1–4, 23–29 (secondary)

### Material facts / procedural posture
Civilian Railways running staff who retired before 1986 disputed a later notional pension fixation and whether running allowance should be counted again.

### Questions before the Court
Whether running allowance could be included twice and whether differences in retirement cohorts violated Article 14.

### Historical statutory framework
Indian Railway Establishment Code Rule 2544(g); Railway Board pension instructions; Article 14

### Narrow holding candidate (not yet JAG validated)
Judgment text reproductions indicate the Court upheld the Railway Board clarification against double-counting and distinguished lawful scheme cut-off dates from arbitrary divisions of a genuinely homogeneous pensioner class.

### Final disposition and relief
Transferred matter allowed, CAT order set aside; amounts already disbursed not to be recovered. This is based on accessible transcription, not an archived authenticated PDF.

### Temporal and factual limits
NOT an Army Act, military discipline or armed-forces disability-pension authority. In a commander's assistant it should be excluded from default Army-law search and retained, if at all, as civilian service-law comparison.
