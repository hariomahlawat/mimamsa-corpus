### Material facts / procedural posture
An ex-Havildar challenged rejection of disability pension after medical invalidment where the medical attribution opinion was contested.

### Questions before the Court
Whether unexplained medical findings and existing disability-pension presumptions entitled the claimant to relief and rounding off.
