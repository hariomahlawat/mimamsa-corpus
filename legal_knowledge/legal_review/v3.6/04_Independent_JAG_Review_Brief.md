## Two-person legal validation protocol
1. **First verifier, primary authorities:** establish court, case number, decision date, judicial authorship, opinion vs headnote, material facts, issue, precise ratio, paragraph/page and operative relief. For third-party and SCR reproductions, flag original-byte parity.
2. **Second independent verifier, conflicting law:** examine every material amendment and temporal application, all known subsequent Supreme Court/competent bench rulings, negative and positive treatment, and any unresolved source textual discrepancy. Evidence from one repeated compilation family is not independent authentication.
3. **Reconciliation:** each disagreement receives exception ID, quoted source location (do not copy whole judgments), proposed correction, supporting primary authority, date and an explicit reviewer disposition.
4. **JAG:** approve, qualify, reject or return each proposition separately, with officer identity, authority/delegation, date, limitations, validity as-of date and recheck date. Case-level broad approval is insufficient.
5. **Database release:** technical gate refuses operational advice unless verified source, legal holding, later precedent treatment, temporal statutory regime and JAG approval have explicit affirmative entries. Refuse/redirect disputed Rule23(3), pre-Anvar Sec65B, date-dependent AFSPA use, 2026 pension claims without timeline checks.
