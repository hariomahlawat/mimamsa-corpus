# v2.9 — Army Rule 23(3) wording and Supreme Court precedent verification

As-of: 2026-11-22. Status: RESEARCH_PENDING_JAG.

This batch adds a *judicial corroboration* tier to the Rule 23(3) disputed caution, and two further original Supreme Court judgment archival candidates.

## Strong finding / limited certification
The 2008 Supreme Court decision **Bachan Singh v Union of India**, reported [2008] 10 SCR 668 (court text mirrored online), specifically mentions the accused being warned that he was **not obliged** to make a confessional statement, with record of Rule23 compliance. The 2015 judgment **Om Prakash v Union of India**, Criminal Appeal 1112/2011, reproduced by Indian Kanoon, quotes the caution containing “not obliged” (paras 11–14). This corroborates the user-uploaded MML PDF's text and makes the omitted negation in two hosted compilation copies a high-risk discrepancy.

**NOT established**: the exact official text of 27 November 1954 S.R.O. 484, a full chain of later amendments, or a gazetted corrigendum. The original Gazette scan was not obtained. The doctrine of voluntary testimony is independently supported by judicial reasoning; that does not resolve a specific Gazette transcription dispute.
