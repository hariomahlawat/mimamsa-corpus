# Original Army Rules Gazette acquisition instruction — v3.2

**Status (22 November 2026): original Gazette images NOT obtained. No legislative-text authentication or JAG approval.**

## Principal requested source

- Notification: **S.R.O. 484, dated 27 November 1954**, made under Section 191 of the Army Act, 1950.
- Publication: **Gazette of India, Part II, Section 4**, issue dated **27 November 1954**, reportedly pp. 291–378, No. 47.
- Essential scan: Original Gazette issue cover/contents, opening notification, Rule 22, **Rule 23(3)** and any first-edition errata/corrigenda, Rules 180/182, final page/identifier. Obtain the entire relevant publication if feasible.
- Specific doubt: Several MoD/India Code/AFT-hosted compiled editions omit the negative **not** in the warning before an accused's statement under Rule 23(3). The user's uploaded MML and court-judgment quotations include it. The omitted negative is operationally hazardous: do **not** use a reading which implies compulsory self-incriminating statement.

## Authorised discovery channels

1. [Central Secretariat Library historical Gazette notice](https://csl.nic.in/page/innerpage/gazette-of-india.php) says the 1950–2002 Gazette archive was digitised and uploaded to the Department of Publication for online search. This is **not** evidence of retrieval of this specific 1954 issue.
2. Search [Government of India eGazette](https://egazette.gov.in/) by **Weekly Gazette**, Part II, Section 4, issue date 27 November 1954. If no result, vary category and record search parameters/screenshots; do not infer nonpublication from a failed electronic query.
3. If not discoverable, request a source image or certified copy from the **Department of Publication / Central Secretariat Library**, specifying S.R.O. 484, date, Part II, Section 4, No.47, page range, and the exact sub-rule 23(3) wording query.
4. Compare against any *separate* original corrigendum and successive Gazette amendments. An annotated compilation hosted on a government site is not automatically an original Gazette copy.

## Supplementary government-hosted compilation (not decisive)

The [Armed Forces Tribunal website version of Army Rules](https://aft.gov.in/public/upload_act_rule_document/1743584005.pdf) also prints Rule 23(3) without **not** in the caution. It contains numerous historical editorial amendments (e.g. S.R.O. 188/1979) and is not identified as a photographic image of the original 27 November 1954 Gazette. Its possible common text ancestry with the India Code copy requires examination. More copies of the same compilation do **not** cure the disputed source wording.

## Evidence intake and approval requirements

For each Gazetted source capture `instrument_id`, Gazette issue/part/section/pages, notification no., issuing department, raw PDF/image bytes, `sha256`, source URL/physical archive reference, date of issuance and commencement, exact provision change, predecessor and successor text, reviewer and review date. Mark `version_as_of` and `jag_approved=false` until legal review.

Do not mark a research claim `AUTHENTICATED_CURRENT_LAW` merely because source bytes were uploaded with matching checksums. Keep all original MML sources immutable.
