# Indian Military Law audit v3.0 — Gazette provenance and source integrity

As-of 2026-11-22, all data herein is **research not JAG approved**.

## What was established
* S.R.O. 484 dated 27 November 1954 is the original notification for Army Rules, 1954. Gazette Part II, Section 4, page 291 is cited by an India Code-hosted edition of the Army Act; pp.291–378 are quoted in a secondary reproduction. **The original signed/scanned Gazette pages have NOT been acquired.**
* The Delhi High Court decision `Subedar Major S.N. Thakur v Union of India` (20 Nov 1998, Criminal Writ 953 and 954/1998) expressly discusses 1993 amendments to Rules 22 and 182, effective 6 December 1993, and the Rule 180 participation condition for dispensing with Rule 22(1). Its exact online transcript was read; original Delhi HC PDF is pending.
* S.R.O. 6(E) dated 6 June 2022, in a visually inspected two-page Gazette facsimile, affects **Rule 16A**, not Rules 22, 23, 180 or 182; a publication note cites S.R.O. 17(E) of 28 December 2019. This 2019 instrument must be distinguished from **S.R.O. 17(E) of 6 December 1993**.
* India Code/AFT-hosted Army Rule 23(3) text omits the word "not" in the accused-statement caution; other copies and judgments include it. Preserve this as a **critical unresolved textual conflict**, never as a silently repaired official text.

## Source authenticity ratings
* Published scanned original Gazette signed/retrieved from official portal: **not yet available for 1954 / 1993 / 2002 / 2019**.
* Later official Gazette *facsimile* 2022 from third-party mirror: read visually, but official-site file hash not compared.
* Rule compilations and manual commentary: may contain later notes, errors and mixed editions.
* Court recitals help identify amendment timelines; they do not substitute for the original notified instrument.
