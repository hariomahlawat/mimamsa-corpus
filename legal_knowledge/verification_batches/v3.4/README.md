## Material 2026 Supreme Court development

*Union of India v Col. N.C. Isaac (Retd.)*, **2026 INSC 993**, decided **15 September 2026**, discusses the 1982 and 2008 Entitlement Rules. Paragraph 39 dismisses the Union's appeals but directs careful examination of Medical Board reasoning rather than routine judicial substitution, and describes the burden shift for claims brought after 15 years under Rule 7 of the 2008 rules. Paragraphs 21–30 require particular care regarding the statutory or executive status of the 2008 scheme. **Do not simplistically describe the 1982 scheme as abolished or the 2008 scheme as wholly inoperative without reading those paragraphs and the operative directions together.**

The seven pension cases in `02_Pension_2026_Treatment_Queue.csv` require case-by-case review against Isaac. This is a **potential relevant later case**, not a blanket finding that all seven older cases are overruled, distinguished or affirmed.
