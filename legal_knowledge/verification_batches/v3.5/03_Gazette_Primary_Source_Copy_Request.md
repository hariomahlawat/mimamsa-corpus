# Archival-copy request: Gazette of India, 27 November 1954

**Prepared for authorised submission through the appropriate government records channel. Not sent.**

**To:** Controller of Publications / Department of Publication, Government of India, or the archival custodian identified by the Central Secretariat Library.

**Subject: Request for authenticated archival copy of Gazette of India, Part II, Section 4, dated 27 November 1954, containing S.R.O. 484 (Army Rules, 1954)**

1. It is requested that a certified or custodian-authenticated scanned copy of the **Gazette of India, Part II, Section 4, issue No. 47 dated 27 November 1954**, containing Ministry of Defence notification **S.R.O. 484**, may be provided. The identified published page range is **pp. 291–378**.

2. The copy is required for archival verification of the **Army Rules, 1954**, particularly the exact wording of **Rule 23(3)** relating to the caution before an accused person's statement. Different later compilations reproduce the warning with materially different wording, including whether the negative word **“not”** appears. It is important that the text be checked against the original publication, without relying on edited compilations.

3. The scan may kindly include the publication header, Gazette issue date/part/section, S.R.O. number, all pages of the notified Rules or at least Rules **22, 23, 180 and 182**, and any contemporaneous **errata or corrigenda**. If the original is available only in a bound volume, the precise archive reference and page numbers may be intimated.

4. If available, authenticated copies of subsequent amendments affecting these provisions are also sought, including **S.R.O. 17(E) dated 6 December 1993** and any amendments/corrigenda relevant to Rule 23(3), with commencement dates. Later printed consolidations are not substitutes for the original instruments.

5. The material will be used solely to establish statutory provenance and maintain an accurate research reference. Publication will remain subject to legal scrutiny and applicable restrictions.

**Details to preserve with the response:** record custodian, register or archival number, source URL or certified copy identifier, page images, issue date, statutory authority, exact text, file SHA-256, date received and person verifying.
