# Seven older pension cases and binding 2026 INSC 993: source-derived review v3.5

**NOT JAG-approved.** This review is based on full page-addressable extract from the stored 2015–2016/1996/2005–2008 original case PDF copies (source file SHA-256 previously checked) and direct reading of the Court's official 93-page 2026 INSC 993 decision.

## What was actually checked
- **Expressly referred to in the 2026 Supreme Court text:** Manjeet Singh, Angad Singh Titaria, Baljit Singh, S. Balachandran Nair, Surinder Singh Rathore.
- **Not expressly named, although potentially legally relevant:** Mani Ram Bhaira and Keshar Singh.
- **Indirect case-law chain:** Keshar Singh (2007) is expressly cited on p10 of Surinder Singh Rathore (2008), which is itself discussed on p51 of the 2026 NC Isaac judgment. This is a citation path, **not a conclusion that Keshar Singh is directly affirmed or overruled by Isaac**.
- **Different dispositions:** Baljit Singh (1996) Supreme Court *dismissed* the Union appeal despite criticism of the High Court's reasoning; S Balachandran Nair (2005), Keshar Singh (2007) and Surinder Singh Rathore (2008) Government appeals were *allowed* with protection against recovery of pensions already paid; Manjeet Singh (2015) and Angad Singh Titaria (2015) Government appeals were *dismissed*; Mani Ram Bhaira (2016) claimant's appeal was *allowed*. Different medical facts, relief, evidential standards and entitlement-rule dates matter.
- **Court reasoning versus counsel:** The sentence in NC Isaac p69 para19.4 that several cases 'continue to hold the field' occurs in respondents' submissions, not in the Court's final ratio. The Court's own discussion in p61 still cites Manjeet and Angad on the requirement for reasons in Medical Board opinions. It does not mean the whole of every old judgment is reaffirmed.
- **New source conflict:** Original Angad Singh Titaria 2015 SCR says **Indian Air Force** Pension Regulations Reg153. The 2026 Isaac reference on p61 describes it as Regulation 153 of the **Army** pension regulations. Both exact original source records are preserved; this conflict is a reviewer observation, not a correction made to either judgment.
- **Final 2026 guidance:** NC Isaac 2026 para39 on pp92–93 stresses due weight to reasons of medical board and 15-year claimant-onus exception. Assess each case's rule version, discharge/claim date and circumstances. The exact status and effect of the 2008 scheme must be read with paras21–30; do not declare the entire scheme null solely from para22.
