# Source hierarchy: pointer

Superseded by **`SOURCE_AUTHORITY_POLICY.md` (v1)**, the owner's binding policy. A parallel draft by the orchestrator (2026-11-22) introduced an `ARCHIVAL_COPY_OF_OFFICIAL` class and a `custody_checks` field. Both were **withdrawn** on 2026-11-22 to avoid two classification schemes. Use the v1 fields instead:
- `document_authority`
- `carrier_provenance`
- `copy_fidelity`
- `amendment_chain_status`
- `instrument_identity`
- `authority_rationale`
- `page_image_check_id`
- the `ARCHIVAL_FACSIMILE_OF_OFFICIAL` authenticity value

The owner's instruction of 2026-11-22 restated in that policy: determine authority, provenance, applicable date and amendment history for every conflicting document; prefer the authenticated original instrument over later transcriptions; government-issued manuals outweigh third-party commentary but never override Acts, Rules, amendments or binding judgments; archival Gazette reproductions are copies of primary documents, not commentary.
