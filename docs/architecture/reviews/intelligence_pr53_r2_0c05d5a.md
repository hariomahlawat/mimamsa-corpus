## 1. Executive assessment

R2 materially improves the proposal. It defines a withdrawal fence and an explicit limit on recalling bytes already transferred. It moves verification after synthesis and binds authorized claims to a frozen artifact. It also separates release records, site visibility and future agent state. The single-host modular approach, direct research path and gated legal engine remain appropriate.

**Nine original findings are closed-confirmed at the architecture level. Eight are partially addressed**, including both original P1s. The most important outstanding decisions are the precise boundary at which a Windows transport accepts protected bytes, how recovery proves the latest accepted state after independently restored components, and how the gateway authenticates a verified artifact and preserves the meaning of its final presentation. The latest A4 and A5 producer contracts also require a deliberate adapter boundary with the application's R1 interchange. The resulting work is largely specification and acceptance work. Current Cycle 13 delivery can continue under its existing gates.

## 3. New findings and residual design decisions

The new IDs identify concrete failure modes exposed by the proposed corrections or the newer producer evidence. The two original P1s remain partially addressed. The severities below describe the separate deficiencies rather than counting those P1s twice.

### R2-N01 · P2 · Handoff acceptance has no concrete transport contract

**Evidence and consequence.** Serving §§1/3 permit in-flight bytes already handed to an OS, socket or proxy to arrive later, while barring unguarded writes from downstream server-managed queues. The distinction is undefined for a controlled IIS proxy, framework pipeline, SignalR/SSE output, file-send primitive or compression middleware. A method may queue a descriptor, copy only part of a chunk or wait on asynchronous backpressure. A guard held until a slow flush can starve withdrawal. Releasing it before a later managed write can defeat the fence. These outcomes cannot be distinguished from the gateway's own sequence log alone. [Serving invariant and protocol](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/0c05d5a00542588cd7bea75f4962837cdde22bdd/docs/architecture/intelligence/SERVING_AND_REVOCATION_CONTRACT.md#L5-L42) · [ADR hosting options](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/9eeb04a457b532563d80610777d873ab24b52da5/docs/architecture/decisions/ADR-0005-APPLICATION-STACK.md#L42-L56) · [ASP.NET response pipes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/aspnet/core/fundamentals/middleware/request-response?view=aspnetcore-10.0) · [ASGI send buffers](https://asgi.readthedocs.io/en/stable/specs/www.html)

**Correction.** Define the ownership transfer for each supported topology and protected surface. Identify the last boundary which may accept each immutable byte slice, whether acceptance is full/partial/absent, who owns retry, and how the independent observation maps to the fence. A native Windows Service with one in-process protected egress and an audited bounded transport adapter is a viable topology to **prove**. For IIS, define whether its downstream stage is inside the fence or a named one-way transport boundary for already accepted bytes. Prohibit downstream caching, replay, deferred file opens and redispatch of protected references. HTTP framework Write/Flush success or a queued send alone is insufficient evidence. Keep expensive identity/closure preparation outside the guard, match revisions and admit byte slices under it. Declare per-connection and aggregate in-flight caps, writer priority at update admission and a bounded fence SLO under stated load. Define 206/range/reconnect semantics, headers, retry and termination for a revoked mid-response PDF or export. Use bounded authenticated polling for protected progress where a stream adapter is unproven. Preserve bounded search and PDF access on an accepted topology.

**Acceptance.** On supported Windows topology, compare independent transport-boundary observations to fence and application sequence traces using synthetic markers. Pause at buffer, partial acceptance, cancellation and retry boundaries under slow clients and concurrent requests. Cover PDF.js ranges and fallback, conditional requests, compression, proxy buffering, response headers, SSE/reconnect and generated downloads. Require zero affected application handoffs after the fence, a measured cap on residual already-accepted bytes, bounded withdrawal latency and normal unrelated access. Report client receipt separately. Extend existing P1/P4/P5/P8 evidence where in scope, then repeat for later protected surfaces.

### R2-N02 · P2 · Recovery requires a defined acceptance record and restore authority

**Evidence and consequence.** Serving §4 describes atomic durable acceptance with an independent high-water and separately discusses restoring an old policy store. It specifies several single-record crash cases, but gives no protocol when the accepted event and independent witness disagree or both are restored to matching older states. A matching old pair cannot establish currency offline. If either write is treated as definitive without a recovery rule, the system can lose an accepted withdrawal or remain closed without a defined way to recover. R1's helper compares the newest pair to a given DB sequence. It supplies neither a separate witness nor a transactional production state machine. [Serving §4](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/0c05d5a00542588cd7bea75f4962837cdde22bdd/docs/architecture/intelligence/SERVING_AND_REVOCATION_CONTRACT.md#L46-L59) · [App restore plan](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/9eeb04a457b532563d80610777d873ab24b52da5/docs/architecture/SECURITY_AND_DEPLOYMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md#L198-L210) · [R1 exclusion helper](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/9eeb04a457b532563d80610777d873ab24b52da5/app/contract/mimamsa_contract/exclusions.py#L75-L116)

**Correction.** Specify one authoritative, rollback-excluded acceptance journal and digest chain, or a clear protocol across an event store and an independent witness. In the latter case, durable preparation holds complete recoverable bytes and a proposed epoch. The explicit compare-and-set on the protected witness commits the matching digest. Define when that write is considered durable, what Admin acknowledgment means, and how idempotent retry after a lost acknowledgment recognizes the same accepted operation. Recovery opens only after the exact committed digest and current local history are verified. Define trust and custody for host-loss recovery, including the rule that restoring both an older DB and a matching older policy snapshot cannot prove currency without protected current evidence. Distinguish pending operations from committed-but-missing data, and retain fail-closed recovery when current authority cannot be shown.

**Acceptance.** Use a separate accepted-operation oracle and interrupt each preparation, witness, publication and acknowledgment boundary on the chosen Windows storage. Independently restore older DB, policy/event store and witness images, vary same-epoch digest, replay the acknowledgment and attempt concurrent update. Demonstrate exact state recovery or authorized safe halt, preserved monotonicity and no false success. Integrate with existing P8 scope.

### R2-N03 · P2 · The fence serializes commits without defining exclusion precedence and merge

**Evidence and consequence.** R2 says to stage candidate state, then serialize and update it with a monotone epoch. The signed exclusion channel supplies a **complete current list**, whereas local Admin withdrawal and upstream bundle publication are distinct states. The app design reattaches local uploads after rollback, and the Legal Knowledge Architecture calls for Admin reconciliation when an upstream release withdraws bytes also published locally. A newer sequence, stale precomputed state, or reattached overlay could erase a different channel's denial unless the coordinator derives the effective state at commit. A dependency manifest of returned hits alone also cannot prove that search counts or a claim's coverage are unaffected by a scoped policy change. [Serving §§2–4](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/0c05d5a00542588cd7bea75f4962837cdde22bdd/docs/architecture/intelligence/SERVING_AND_REVOCATION_CONTRACT.md#L11-L48) · [R1 full-state rule](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/9eeb04a457b532563d80610777d873ab24b52da5/app/contract/schemas/v1/exclusion_update.schema.json#L4-L16) · [Release and overlay sequence](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/9eeb04a457b532563d80610777d873ab24b52da5/docs/architecture/SECURITY_AND_DEPLOYMENT_ARCHITECTURE.md#L181-L210) · [Local-source precedence](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/f1682c664d564aa7ad1c317065fafb302afe4ccc/docs/architecture/LEGAL_KNOWLEDGE_ARCHITECTURE.md#L225-L237)

**Correction.** Define the effective policy from independently versioned inputs: signed restriction/withdrawal state, site publication and local withdrawal, active release, owner/session authorizations and legal holds. Re-validate prepared changes against the exact prior component revisions under the same commit and define authorized re-admission for a former restriction or withdrawal. An unrelated update or rollback cannot implicitly re-admit it. Keep upstream bundle-only withdrawal and site-wide hash suppression distinct. Bind aggregate results to an authorized query partition/index revision or a provable dependency certificate. Treat counts, negative coverage statements and indirect aliases as dependencies, with conservative epoch invalidation when a narrow closure cannot be proven.

**Acceptance.** Start a signed update and local withdrawal over the same old epoch in both orders. Test both a conflicting same-sequence digest and two independent updates. Reattach an alternate local publication on rollback, then verify explicit authorized re-admission and unrelated continuity. Exercise query caches/result counts and a coverage-dependent claim whose evidence set changes even though no previously displayed hit changed.

### R2-N04 · P2 · Immutable claim content needs trusted issuance and a final presentation contract

**Evidence and consequence.** R2 requires a frozen content tree, versioned renderer and digest. A digest ties bytes to a record, but does not establish who issued that record. CLAIM-07 demands rejection of a forged verifier record without specifying the trusted issuance and lookup path. The renderer rule protects claim text and material qualifications but permits layout, accessibility labels and approved templates. A heading, table association, visual redaction, screen reader order or export field update can change what a user understands without changing any atomic claim string. Source-only fallback still permits material questions and gaps, which become an uncontrolled text channel if filled by the failed synthesis. The Agentic diagram also places rule evaluation upstream of the gate and bypasses pre-context authorization on the evidence path, even though the normative prose correctly requires it. [Final Claims §§2–4/6](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/0c05d5a00542588cd7bea75f4962837cdde22bdd/docs/architecture/intelligence/FINAL_CLAIM_VERIFICATION_CONTRACT.md#L9-L45) · [CLAIM-07](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/0c05d5a00542588cd7bea75f4962837cdde22bdd/docs/architecture/intelligence/FINAL_CLAIM_VERIFICATION_CONTRACT.md#L54-L69) · [Agentic diagram and rule](https://github.com/hariomahlawat/mimamsa-corpus/blob/0c05d5a00542588cd7bea75f4962837cdde22bdd/docs/architecture/intelligence/AGENTIC_AI_ARCHITECTURE.md#L9-L29)

**Correction.** Make authorization records server-issued, with an access-controlled store or authenticated service envelope, canonical encoding, binding to issuer, actor/session, class, fact revision, provenance, trusted verifier result and template/renderer version. The delivery gateway resolves trusted records and computes integrity against those records. Keep typed qualifier, attribution, ordering, pairing and heading relationships in the authorized presentation tree, including alternative/accessible views. Constrain rendering and inspect semantic output of PDF/DOCX as appropriate, including embedded comments, metadata and mutable fields. Keep source-only fallback server-composed from approved references, fixed status phrases and vetted structured questions. Give explicitly user-authored unverified workspace content its own provenance and routes. Bring the diagram into agreement with both gates.

**Acceptance.** Test internal worker and direct API requests with self-consistent unauthorized envelopes, wrong-owner authentic artifacts and stale verifier attestations. Compare compact, detailed, screen-reader, print, PDF and DOCX meanings against the same independent oracle. Include a loss of attribution or conditional heading while text remains byte-identical, a template-generated relationship and placeholders. Force fallback after rejection and exercise fact or approval change before preview and every subsequent handoff. Supported unchanged content and explicitly separate user notes have positive controls.

### R2-N05 · P2 · The graph matrix covers R1 without mapping the current A4/A5 producers

**Evidence and consequence.** A5 delivers its own IC-01-labelled graph with structural, judicial and forms relations beyond the R1 enum, nullable source/page in some classes, and documentary-only review axes. Its provision-time interface describes no provision-version nodes at that build. A4 now supplies separate candidate version records with sequence-based IDs, evidence references, independently qualified dates, disputed commencement and retrospective effects. The proposed graph matrix covers R1 but maps neither producer.
