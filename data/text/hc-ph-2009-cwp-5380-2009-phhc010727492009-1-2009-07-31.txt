PERMOD KOHLI.J
  (ORAL)

It is admitted case of the parties that the issue involved in the

present petition is covered by a judgement of the Hon'ble Supreme Court,

passed in Civil Appeal No. 6587 of 2008 titled as Union of India & others

Vs. Rajpal Singh, followed by a Division Bench of this Court in CWP No.

17911 of 2007 decided on 11.12.2008, wherein following observations have

been made:-

“ In view of the above, it is held that the discharge of the

petitioner was not in accordance with the prescribed procedure.

Accordingly, we all all these writ petitions, set aside the discharge of the

petitioners and direct the respondents to re-instate the petitioners in service

with all consequential benefits.  The respondents are, however, not

precluded from holding the Invaliding Medical Boards after such joining in

accordance with law as per the Army Act, 1950, The Army Rules, 1954 and

Army Instructions.”
