1.
The present petition has been filed for quashing the impugned order

dated 09.01.2025 (Annexure P1) passed by respondent No.1, wherein it was held

that only the pensioner’s widow is entitled to the family pension and the husband

of the petitioner was not a pensioner.

2.
After arguing for some time, learned counsel for the petitioner

submits that the present petition may kindly be disposed of having been not

pressed  as the husband of the petitioner was not a pensioner so as to entitle the

petitioner with family pension after his death.

3.
Ordered accordingly.
