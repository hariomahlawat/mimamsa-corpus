Court No. - 5

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 9830 of 2021

Petitioner :- Pradeep Kumar
Respondent :- Union Of India And Another
Counsel for Petitioner :- Saurabh Kumar Pandey
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Saral Srivastava,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and
learned counsel for the respondents.

The petitioner, by means of this writ petition, has
prayed for quashing  the impugned orders dated
19.1.2021 and 12.2.2021 passed by respondent
no.2.

A preliminary objection has been raised by Mrs.
Seema Singh, learned counsel for the respondents
that the petitioner has statutory alternative
remedy of filing original application before the
Armed Forces Tribunal and as such, the writ
petition is not maintainable.

Since there is statutory alternative remedy
available to the petitioner under the Act, therefore,
the writ petition is not maintainable and is,
accordingly, dismissed on the ground of
availability of alternative remedy.

Order Date :- 11.8.2021
