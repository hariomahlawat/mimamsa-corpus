RITU BAHRI, J. (ORAL)

This writ petition pertains to pension of EX-Army

personnel. At the outset, learned counsel for the Union of India has

stated that all service matters concerning members of Armed Forces

involving pension and other retiral benefits pending before this Court,

are required to be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal,

Chandigarh Bench, Chandigarh (for brevity 'The Army Tribunal”),

which is constituted for this purpose under Armed Forces Tribunal

Act, 2007. This Writ petition has been filed under Articles 226/227 of

the Constitution of India challenging the order passed by the

Administrative Tribunal, which now can be heard by the Army

Tribunal.

After hearing learned counsel for the Union of India, I am

of the view that this matter is liable to be transferred to the Army

Tribunal.

I order accordingly.

This matter along with original record be sent to the
