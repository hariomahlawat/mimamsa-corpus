Hon'ble Saurabh Srivastava,J.

Heard learned counsel for the parties.

The present petition has been filed for the following reliefs:

"i. Issue a writ order or direction in the nature of mandamus by commanding
and directing to the respondents to disburse the entire benefits to the
petitioner in the light of Succession certificate dated 03.01.2023 as claimed
under Application dated 21.03.2023 (Annexure No. 07 to the writ petition).

ii. Issue any writ order or direction in any nature in the facts and
circumstances of the case narrated in the present case."

The instant petition is not maintainable in pursuance to the Armed
Forces Tribunal Act, 2007. The grievance as raised by the
petitioner is well covered by the remedy as defined under the Act
itself.

The instant writ petition stands disposed of on the ground of
alternative remedy available to the petitioner by way of raising all
his grievances under Section 21 of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act,
2007.
