25.02.2020
1. This petition was entertained at a time when the Chairperson of the

Armed Forces Tribunal („AFT‟) had not been appointed to replace the

incumbent who had retired. Since then, a Chairman of the AFT has been

appointed and all matters are now being entertained in the AFT in

accordance with law.

2. The Court therefore permits the present Petitioner to now approach the

AFT, not later than four weeks from today, by filing an appropriate

application in accordance with law, and if necessary, also invoke Rule 6 of

the Armed Forces Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, since the cause of action

arose in Jabalpur, where, even as on date, there is no functioning Bench of

the AFT.

3. It is clarified that it will be open to the parties to rely on the pleadings in

[[page 2]]
the present petition before the AFT.  The interim order already passed by the

Court is directed to continue till such time as the AFT passes appropriate

orders.

4. The petition and application are disposed of.
