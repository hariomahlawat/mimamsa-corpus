Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

Learned Standing Counsel has obtained instructions and filed
before this Court, which are taken on record.

A preliminary objection has been raised by Sri R.K.singh,
learned counsel for the respondent regrading maintainability
of this petition as prayer made in respect of certain amount is
an incidence of service, and therefore, matter is cognizable by
Armed Force Tribunal constituted under Armed Forces Tribunal
Act, 2007.

In views of above, I decline to entertain this petition, however,
liberty rests with the petitioner to approach Armed Force
Tribunal, if so advised.

The Writ petition is consigned to records.
