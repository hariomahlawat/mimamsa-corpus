AUGUSTINE GEORGE MASIH, J. (ORAL)

It is the contention of the petitioner that he was boarded out of

service on medical grounds being 100% disabled and therefore, is entitled

to disability pension which he has not been granted till date.

Claiming the said benefit, petitioner had been representing the

respondents and one of the representation dated 12.04.1988 (Annexure

P-3) has been appended with the present writ petition.  Retiral benefits

being a right which accrues to a person every month grant him recurring

cause of action and delay, if any, would not affect his claim.

Accordingly, counsel for the petitioner prays that the petitioner

at this stage would be satisfied if a direction is issued to the Director

General, Border Roads, Kashmir House, DHQ P.O. New Delhi-respondent

No.2 to consider and decide his representation dated 12.04.1988

(Annexure P-3) within some specified time.

Without going into the merits of the case or commenting

thereon, this writ petition is disposed of with a direction to the Director

General, Border Roads, Kashmir House, DHQ P.O. New Delhi-respondent

No.2 to consider the claim of the petitioner as made by him through his

representation dated 12.04.1988 (Annexure P-3) and decide the same

within a period of four months from the date of receipt of certified copy of
