This writ petition, in our opinion should not be entertained.

The petitioner had impugned order dated 11th January, 2017 passed by

the Senior Record Officer, Officer In Charge, Record Office Army

Ordinance Corps, Secunderabad, before the Armed Forces Tribunal,

Principal Bench, New Delhi in O.A. No. 317/2017.  By order dated 6th

March, 2017, this O.A was dismissed observing that the Tribunal was

not inclined to interfere as no cause of indulgence on any account was

established.  A review filed by the petitioner was also dismissed vide

order dated 2nd May, 2017.

The petitioner has now approached the High Court in this writ

petition under Article 226 impugning the same order dated 11th

January, 2017.  This is impermissible and the writ is not maintainable.

The petitioner, if aggrieved by the orders dated 6th March, 2017 and

2nd May, 2017 passed by the Principal Bench of the Armed Forces,

[[page 2]]
should take recourse with the proceedings in accordance with law.

With the aforesaid observations, we do not entertain the present

writ petition and the same is dismissed.
