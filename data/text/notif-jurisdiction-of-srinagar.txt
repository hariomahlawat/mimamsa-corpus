NOTIFICATION

New Delhi, the 22nd March, 2017

S.R.O. 4(E).— In partial modification of the notification of the Government of India in the Ministry
of Defence, S.R.O. 17 (E), dated 10th November, 2009, published in the Gazettee of India, Extraordinary, Part-
II, Section 4, the Central Government hereby notifies the jurisdiction of the Bench of the Armed Forces
Tribunal at Chandigarh with effect from the 17th day of November, 2016 to the territorial limits of the States
of Punjab, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh and the Union Territory of Chandigarh only.

[F. No. 5(5)/2010-D(AFTC)]
