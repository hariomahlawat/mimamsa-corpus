MINISTRY OF DEFENCE

NOTIFICATION

New Delhi, the 2% July, 2011

8.R.0. 05(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred by clauses (f), (g) and (k) of sub-section (2) of Section 41

of the Armed Forces Tribunat Act, 2007 (55 of 2007), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules to
amend the Armed Forces Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 2008, namely :—

1. Short title and Commencement.—(1) These rules may be called the Armed Forces Tribunal (Procedure)
Amendment Rules, 2011.
B

(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

2.
Inthe Armed Forces Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, 2008, (hereinafter referred to as the Principal Rules), (a)

Rules 7 shall be re-numbered, as sub-rule (1) thereof and after sub-rule (1) of rules 7 as so re-numbered, the following
sub-rule shall be inserted, namely :—

*(2) — Notwithstanding anything contained in sub-rule (1), the application fee referred to in that sub-

rule
shall be payable only in respect of—

()
Original Application (OA);

(i) Review Application (RA);

(iify Contempt Application (CA);

{iv) Application for restoration of Petition (MA); and

(v) Application for transfer of case (from one Bench to another or from one court to another in the

same Bench) (AT)”.

3.
After rule 12 of the principal rules, the following sub-rule shall be inserted, namely
:
—

“12A.—E’very application under rule 8 and the reply under rule 12 shall be supported by an affidavit of

the person verifying the application or reply, as the case may be.”
