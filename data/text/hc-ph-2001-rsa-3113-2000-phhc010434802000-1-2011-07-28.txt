K.C. Puri, J.

CM No. 306-C of 2011 has been filed for transferring the

case to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench, Chandigarh.

Counsel for the parties have stated at the Bar that the issue

involved in the present appeal is cognizable by the Armed Forces

Tribunal constituted under Section 4 of the Armed Forces Tribunal

Act, 2007, by the Central Government, vide notification dated

7.8.2009.

So, application stands allowed and the present appeal stands

transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench,

Chandigarh.

Parties through their counsel are directed to appear before

the abovesaid Tribunal on 2.9.2011.
