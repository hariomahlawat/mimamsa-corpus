1.
Challenge in the present writ petition is to the order dated

11.05.2023 passed by the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh (for short

‘the AFT’) in O.A. No.1529 of 2018 titled as “Suraj Bhan Vs. Union of

India and others” whereby directions were issued to the petitioners to

release the payment to the applicant-respondent No.2 on the basis of One

Rank One Pension (OROP).

2.
The issue raised in the present petition is no more res-integra

in view of the judgment rendered by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in Civil

Appeal Diary No(s).31127 of 2022 titled as “Union of India and others

Vs. Lt. Cdr. Mathew Joseph (Retd.) and others, decided on 12.12.2022,

wherein the Apex Court has held as under:-

“Application seeking permission to file appeal

is allowed.

Delay is condoned.

We do not find any good ground and reason to

interfere with the impugned judgments/orders, as they

do not pass or issue any affirmative direction, but

record that the matter is pending before the anomalies

committee.

We request the anomalies committee to decide

the issue in question expeditiously and preferably

within a period of four months form the date of receipt

of copy of this order.

Recording the aforesaid, the civil appeals are

dismissed.

Pending application(s), if any, stand disposed

of.”

3.
In view thereto, we do not find any reason to interfere with the

impugned order dated 11.05.2023 passed by the AFT and the same be now

complied within a period of three months from today.

4.
The present writ petition is, accordingly, dismissed.
