SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA J.(Oral)

1.
The challenge in this writ-petition is to the order passed by

respondent No.2-Armed Forces Tribunal (for short ‘the AFT’) on

06.12.2022 (Annexure P-1).

2.
Taking into consideration the fact that the petitioners had

challenged the order dated 06.12.2022 by preferring a writ-petition in the

year 2025 before this Court and no cogent reasons have come up for the

inordinate delay in approaching this Court.

3.
The Hon’ble Supreme Court in “Thirunagalingam vs.

Lingeswaran and another” 2025 INSC 672, has observed that delay should

not be condoned merely on generosity and bonafides of explanation is

necessary. Therefore, we would not accept the reasons coming forward for

the inordinate delay.
