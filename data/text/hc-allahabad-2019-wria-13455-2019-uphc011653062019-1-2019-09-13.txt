Court No. - 38

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 13455 of 2019

Petitioner :- Neeraj Sharma
Respondent :- Union Of India And 6 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Rajesh Kumar Roy Sharma
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Jyotsna Srivastava

Hon'ble Ashwani Kumar Mishra,J.

Petitioner was employed in the Indian Navy.

In respect of his grievance, the petitioner has a remedy of
approaching Armed Forces Tribunal, which has not been
invoked. This petition, therefore, is not liable to be entertained
in view of the law laid down by the Apex Court in L. Chandra
Kumar Vs. Union of India, (1997) 3 SCC 261.

Objection made by Ms. Jyotsna Srivastava on behalf of
respondents is liable to be sustained.

Writ petition, accordingly, is dismissed.

Order Date :- 13.9.2019
