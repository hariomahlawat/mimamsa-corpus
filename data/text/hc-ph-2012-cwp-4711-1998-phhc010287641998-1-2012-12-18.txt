The relief claimed in the present writ petition is of

promotion to the rank of Subedar, which would be within the

jurisdiction of the  Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the

Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandi Mandir for further disposal.

The writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
