SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA J.(Oral)

The challenge in this writ-petition is to the order passed by

respondent No.2-Armed Forces Tribunal (for short ‘the AFT’) dated

07.03.2019 (Annexure P-1).

2.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that there was no

occasion to round off the disability from 30% to 50%.

3.
The issue is no more res-integra in view of the judgment

passed by the Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of Bijender Singh vs. Union

of India and others, 2025 SSC OnLine SC 895, which affirms the earlier

judgment passed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case of Union of

India and others vs. Ram Avtar 2014 SCC Online SC 1761 wherein the

Hon’ble Apex Court has approved the rounding off clause relating to grant

of disability pension to 50% where the disability is less than 50%, 75%

where the disability is from 50% to 75% and 100% where the disability is

more than 75%.
