REVIEW PET. 454/2018 in W.P.(C) 3467/2018

1. The order dated 26th November, 2018 is clarified only to the extent that

notwithstanding the observation of this Court that the interim orders of the

AFT dated 8th February and 16th March, 2018 “appear to be perfectly

justified in the facts and circumstances of the case”, since they are only

interlocutory orders, the AFT will decide the OA finally on merits,

uninfluenced by the aforementioned observation of this Court.

2. It is further clarified that in the appearance on behalf of the Respondent-

Union of India as noted in the order dated 26th November, 2018 should read

as “Ms. Jasmeet Singh for Mr. Anurag Ahluwalia, CGSC.”

3.  The review petition is disposed of in the above terms.
