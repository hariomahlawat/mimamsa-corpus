Court No. - 33

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 7574 of 2021

Petitioner :- Thakur Rajendra Singh Sajjan Singh
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Yakub Ansari
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Ashwani Kumar Mishra,J.

Petitioner is claiming relief against the authorities of Armed
Forces.

Ms. Seema Singh, learned counsel appearing for the
respondents raises a preliminary objection on the ground that
remedy of the petitioner would be to approach the Armed
Forces Tribunal.

Objection raised on behalf of respondents is sustained.

Writ petition is, accordingly, dismissed with liberty to petitioner
to approach appropriate forum in respect of his grievance.

Order Date :- 16.7.2021
Ashok Kr.
