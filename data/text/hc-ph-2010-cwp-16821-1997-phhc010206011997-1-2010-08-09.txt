VINOD K. SHARMA, J. (ORAL)

The petitioner has approached this Court by invoking the

writ jurisdiction of this Court under Articles 226/227 of the

Constitution of India, for issuance of writ of mandamus to the

respondents, to give benefit of military service rendered by the

petitioner during the period from 1948 to 1865 i.e. about 17 years,

and to release the pensionary benefits forthwith. The petitioner

further prays for issuance of writ of mandamus directing the

respondents to count the entire service rendered by the petitioner for

grant of pension.

On constitution of the Armed Forces Tribunal, this writ

petition was transferred to the Hon'ble Armed Forces Tribunal,

Regional Bench Chandigarh at Chandimandir.

The Hon'ble Armed Forces Tribunal  Regional Bench

returned the case by passing the following order :-

“It is admitted by both the ld. Counsel for the

parties that the matter under dispute is not

cognizable by the Armed Forces Tribunal.

Accordingly, this application is returned to

the Hon'ble High Court of Punjab And

Haryana, Chandigarh, for further necessary

action.”

The order passed by the Army Tribunal shows, that the

petitioner is not the army personnel, but is civilian employee of the

Union of India, therefore, the jurisdiction to deal with the case is with

the Central Administrative Tribunal, and not with this Court.

Consequently, this writ petition is ordered to be

transferred to the Hon'ble  Central Administrative Tribunal,

Chandigarh.

The parties, through their counsel, are directed to appear

before the Hon'ble  Central Administrative Tribunal, Chandigarh

Bench on 27.9.2010.

Writ petition disposed off accordingly.
