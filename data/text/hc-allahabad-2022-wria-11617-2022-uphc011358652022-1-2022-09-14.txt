Court No. - 32

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 11617 of 2022

Petitioner :- Vikesh Kumar Balmiki
Respondent :- The Union Of India And 5 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Rishabh Kesarwani,Devesh Mishra
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Manjari Singh

Hon'ble Vikram D. Chauhan,J.

The petitioner was Soldier in the Army and he has been
discharged from service. However, the claim of the petitioner
pertains to payment of GPF and other terminal benefits.

Sri Rahul Jain, learned counsel holding brief of Ms. Manjari
Singh, learned counsel for the respondents submits that the
petitioner has an alternate remedy of approaching the Armed
Forces Tribunal.

Confronted with the aforesaid situation, learned counsel for the
petitioner submits that the present writ petition may be
permitted to be withdrawn with the liberty to approach the
Armed Forces Tribunal.

Learned counsel for the respondents has no objection.

Under the circumstances, the present writ petition is dismissed
as withdrawn with the liberty to the petitioner to approach the
Armed Forces Tribunal by filing proper Original Application. In
case any Original Application is filed by the petitioner within a
period of fifteen days from today, the same shall be heard and
decided on merits by the Armed Forces Tribunal.
