Case :- WRIT - A No. - 12564 of 2020

Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Deepak Rana
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Purnendu Kumar
Singh,Sanjai Singh

Hon'ble Yashwant Varma,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, Sri Sanjai Singh who

appears for the third respondent and Sri Purnendu Kumar Singh

for the Union respondents.

Learned counsel appearing for the Union respondents states that

the instant matter relates to pension and would be cognizable by

the concerned Armed Forces Tribunal. In view of the aforesaid

learned counsel for the petitioner prays for and is granted

permission to have the instant petition dismissed as withdrawn

subject to liberty being reserved to approach the appropriate

Tribunal.

Order accordingly.
