LISA GILL.J (Oral)

Applicant-respondent no.1 seeks review of decision dated

27.11.2024 passed in CWP-14736-2024, whereby order dated 24.09.2019

passed by the Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh was

quashed and set aside.

Having heard learned counsel, in our considered opinion,

learned counsel for applicant-respondent no.1 merely seeks to reargue the

matter afresh.  He is unable to point out any error apparent on the face of it

or any other ground which calls for review of decision dated 27.11.2024.  It

is a settled position that Court's jurisdiction to review its judgement is
