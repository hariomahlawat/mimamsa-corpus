Court No. - 36

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 3635 of 2021

Petitioner :- Satish Kumar
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Awadhesh Prasad
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Mahesh Chandra Tripathi,J.

Heard learned counsel for the parties.

Present writ petition has been preferred assailing the validity of
the order dated 1.12.2016 passed by respondent authority by
which "(k) Remarks-Punishment Awarded 07 days Pay Fine and
(d) Punishment-Pay fine" has been awarded/ mentioned against
the petitioner.

At the outset, learned counsel for the respondents raised a
preliminary objection regarding the maintainability of the writ
petition on the ground that the matter is cognizable by Armed
Forces Tribunal and as such the writ petition is liable to be
dismissed.

The Court has proceeded to examine the record in question and
find substance in the objection raised by learned counsel for the
respondents.

Consequently, the writ petition is dismissed.

Order Date :- 25.3.2021
