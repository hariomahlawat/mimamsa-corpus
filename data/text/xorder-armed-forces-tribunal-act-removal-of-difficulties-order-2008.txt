S.R.O. 18(E).—In exercise of the powers conferred

by sub-section (1) of Section 40 of the Armed Forces
Tribunal Act, 2007 (55 of 2007), the Central Government
hereby makes the following Order, namely -—

1. (LD) This Order may be called the Armed Forces
Tribunal Act (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2008.

(2) It shall come into force on the date of its
publication in the Official Gazette.

2. In the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 (55 of
2007), in Section 6, in sub-section (3), in clause (a), for the
word “and” occurring at the end, the word “or” shall be
substituted.
