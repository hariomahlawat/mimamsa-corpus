HARSIMRAN SINGH SETHI , J. (Oral)

1.
In the present petition, challenge is to the impugned order dated

31.01.2019 (Annexure P-1) passed by respondent No.2-Armed Forces

Tribunal, Regional Bench Chandigarh (in short, ‘the Tribunal’) by which,

respondent No.1 has been allowed the benefit of disability pension alognwith

the benefit of rounding off of the disability element @ 50% as against 40%

for life, even though he was discharged from service  being an undesirable

soldier.

2.
Learned counsel for the respondent No. 1 submits that  the issue

raised in the present petition  has already been considered and rejected   by

this  Court while  passing the order in CWP-29083-2025- ‘Union of India

and others versus Ex. Sep.  Bias Dev Singh  and another, decided on

26.09.2025’ and the contentions raised by the learned counsel for the

petitioners  have already been negativated . Hence,  the present petition  may

also be  dismissed in the same terms and conditions.

3.
Learned counsel for the petitioners has not been able to rebut the

aforesaid facts.
