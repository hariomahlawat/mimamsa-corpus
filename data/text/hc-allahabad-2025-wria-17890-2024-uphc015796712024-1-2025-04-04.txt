Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

Petitioner wants consideration of her application for insertion of the name as
wife of the employee of Armed Forces.

Sri Abrar Ahmad, learned counsel for the respondent submits that petitioner
has an alternative efficacious remedy before Armed Forces Tribunal
constituted under Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

Learned counsel for the petitioner could not dispute the same, however, only
she submits that application may be directed to be considered in accordance
with law.

In my considered view, once the Tribunal has been constituted for Armed
Forces to adjudicate service dispute and claims in service matters, it would
more appropriate for the petitioner to apply before Armed Forces Tribunal.

Subject to aforesaid liberty, this petition is consigned to records.
