The petitioner in the present case was serving with the Indian Army.

Learned counsel for the parties do not dispute that the issue raised in the present

petition pertains to the service dispute of the petitioner. Hence, the jurisdiction to

adjudicate upon the same lies with the Armed Force Tribunal (for short “the

Tribunal”). It is prayed further that the matter be transferred to the Tribunal.

In view of the fair stand taken by the learned counsel for the parties, the

present petition is transferred to Armed Forces Tribunal, Srinagar Bench at

Jammu. As submitted by the parties, the Bench is holding its hearing at Jammu

from February 26, 2019 to February 28, 2019.

Learned counsel for the parties to appear before the Tribunal at Jammu on

February 26, 2019.

Original record of the case be transferred to the Tribunal by the Registry

of this court after retaining the photocopy thereof.
