Ashutosh Mohunta, J. (oral)

The petitioner has prayed for quashing the order dated

4.11.2006 (Annexure P-3), vide which his claim with regard to the grant

of disability pension has been rejected.

The petitioner was enrolled in the Army on 27th November,

1965.  He was discharged in pursuance to the orders, passed by the

Medical Board on 11th November, 1975 after having rendered service of

09 years and 350 days.  The petitioner filed an application for grant of

disability pension on 5th April, 2006 and the same has been rejected vide

order dated 4th November, 2006 (Annexure P-3) on the ground that the

petitioner is suffering from “EPILEPSY GRANDAL' which is neither

considered attributable to nor aggravated by Military Service.

Prior to his filing the representation, the petitioner had also

filed a suit for declaration in the Court of Additional Civil Judge (Senior

Division), Samana which was dismissed U/O 9 Rule 8  CPC vide order

[[page 2]]
CWP No.16927 of 2008                                            2

dated 15th January, 2002.

A perusal of the aforementioned facts shows that firstly the

petitioner had filed a civil suit claiming the same relief, which was

dismissed and that order has become final as the petitioner did not file

any appeal etc. against the order (Annexure P-2).

Apart from this, the petitioner has filed this writ petition

after an inordinate delay of about 30 years from the date of his discharge

and his disease i.e. 'EPILEPSY GRANDAL' has not been found

attributable to military service by the Military authorities.

Resultantly, we find no merit in this writ petition and the

same is dismissed.
