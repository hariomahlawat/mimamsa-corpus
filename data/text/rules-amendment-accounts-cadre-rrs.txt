S.R.O. 13(E).—In the notification of Government of India, Ministry of Defence published in the Gazette of

India vide S.R.O.(E) 04 dated 23rd April, 2018 regarding Armed Forces Tribunal (Group-A posts) Recruitment Rules,

2018. In the partial modification of the said rules, the Column No. 4 of Serial No. 7 of Schedule may be read as

“Level-11 (Rs. 67700-208700)” instead of “Level-11 (Rs. 67100-208700)”.
