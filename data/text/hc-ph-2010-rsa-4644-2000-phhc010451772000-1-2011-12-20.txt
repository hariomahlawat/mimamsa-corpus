Rakesh Kumar Garg, J. (Oral)

This appeal pertains to the dispute between the parties

regarding entitlement of pensionary benefits of the appellant who served

with the Indian Army.

After coming into force the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007,

the jurisdiction of this Court is barred and the matter can only be heard by

the Armed Forces Tribunal, as constituted under the aforesaid Act.

In this view of the  matter, this application is allowed.  Let this

appeal be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh, Bench at

Chandimandir.

Disposed of.
