Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

Petitioner wants decision of his application regarding assessment
of basic pay which according to him had been incorrectly fixed.

Petitioner is a retired soldier of Indian Army, as he claims, from
the post of Subedar.

The matters relating to the members of Armed Forces including
those retired are cognizable by Armed Forces Tribunal constituted
under the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

In view of the above, while I decline to entertain this petition,
liberty rests with the petitioner to apply before the Armed Forces
Tribunal, if so advised.

Petition is accordingly consigned to records.
