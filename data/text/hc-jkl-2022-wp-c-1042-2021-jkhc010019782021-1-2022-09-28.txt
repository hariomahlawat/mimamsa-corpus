O R D E R

28.09.2022

This petition stands disposed of by this Court vide Order dated 31st

May, 2021, with a direction to the Registry to transfer the same to the Armed

Forces Tribunal, (AFT), Jammu Bench. It appears that the file was

inadvertently sent to the Central Administrative Tribunal, (CAT), Srinagar

Bench.

The file has been returned  to this Court by the CAT in terms of Order

dated 4th August, 2022, on the ground that the matter pertains to Armed

Forces personnel and, therefore, not amenable to the jurisdiction of CAT.

Be that as it may, in the given circumstances, the Registry should have

of its own transferred this petition to the AFT in compliance to the order

passed by this Court on 31st May, 2021. It seems that the Registry without

applying its mind has put up the disposed of petition before this Court for

consideration.

In view of the aforesaid, the Registry is directed to immediately and

forthwith comply with the Order dated 31st May, 2021 and transfer this

petition to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Jammu Bench.
