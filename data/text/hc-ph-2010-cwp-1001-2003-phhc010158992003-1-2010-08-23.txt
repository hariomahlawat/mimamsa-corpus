RANJIT  SINGH,  J.

The prayer made in the application is for transferring the

writ petition to the Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the Armed

Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

The claim made in the writ petition is for disability

pension.
In view of Section 34 of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007,

the present case is mandatorily required to be transferred to the Tribunal

constituted under the Act. The Registry is directed to transfer the records of

the present petition to the  Armed Forces Tribunal at Chandi Mandir.

Disposed of accordingly.
