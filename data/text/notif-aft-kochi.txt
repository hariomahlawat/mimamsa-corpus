New Delhi, the 2nd December, 2009

S.R.0. 19(E).——In exercise of the powers.conferred

by sub-section (4) of Section 5 of the Armed Forces Tribunal

Act, 2007 (55 of 2007), the Central Government hereby

notifies the Bench of the Armed Forces Tribunal at Kochi

with effect from 7th day of December, 2009, which shall
have jurisdiction within the territorial limits of the States of
Kerala and Karnataka and Union territory of Lakshadweep.
