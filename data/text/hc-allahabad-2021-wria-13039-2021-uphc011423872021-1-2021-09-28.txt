Court No. - 34

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 13039 of 2021

Petitioner :- Smt. Provati Das
Respondent :- Union Of India And 4 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Santosh Kumar Tiwari
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Yashwant Varma,J.

Sri Tripathi, learned counsel appearing for the Union

respondents, takes a preliminary objection to point out that the

instant writ petition which relates to family pension would be

liable to be considered by the competent Armed Forces Tribunal

in terms of the provision made in Section 3(o). Having perused

the aforesaid provision, the preliminary objection is upheld.

The writ petition shall stand dismissed with liberty reserved to

the petitioner to approach the competent Armed Forces

Tribunal.

Order Date :- 28.9.2021
Arun K. Singh
