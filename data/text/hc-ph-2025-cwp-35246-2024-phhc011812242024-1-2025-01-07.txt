SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA, J.(Oral)

Admittedly, respondent No.1 had been granted disability pension

on the recommendation of the Medical Board and was getting the same @ 20%

from 01.05.1991 to 31.05.2001 whereafter the writ petitioners stopped his

disability pension. The Armed Forces Tribunal has directed for constituting

Re-Survey Medical Board and accordingly to take decision and release the

arrears restricted to 03 years.

2.
The UOI is assailing the said order before us submitting that

after 2001, respondent No.1 never demanded the Re-Survey Medical Board.

3.
We find the arguments to be without any basis and find that the

claim of respondent No.1 has been limited to period of 03 years alone which

would cover the limitation period. In view thereto, no interference is warranted

and the present writ petition is accordingly dismissed.
