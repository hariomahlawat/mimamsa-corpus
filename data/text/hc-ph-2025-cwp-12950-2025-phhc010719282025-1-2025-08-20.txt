Harsimran Singh Sethi, J. (Oral)

1.
By these petitions, the prayer of the petitioners is to give

appropriate directions to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench,

Chandigarh (hereinafter referred to as ‘the Tribunal), to decide the execution

applications filed by the petitioners so as to execute the orders passed by the

Tribunal extending certain benefits in favour of the petitioners.

2.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that the respondent-

authorities did not implement the impugned orders within the stipulated time

period of three months, as had been directed by the Tribunal, therefore, the

execution applications was filed by the petitioners, direction to dispose of

which applications is being sought by the petitioners.

3.
Notice of motion.

4.
On asking of this Court, Ms. Bhavana Datta, Senior Panel

Counsel, appears and accepts notice on behalf of respondent – Union of India.

5.
Learned counsel for the respondent has not been able to dispute

with regard to the aforesaid factual position.

6.
We have heard learned counsel for the parties and have gone

through the record with their able assistance.

7.
Considering the nature of relief being sought in the writ petitions
