By way of this writ petition, the petitioners sought quashing of order

dated 10th July 2008 passed by respondent No. 5 directing the recovery of

SCCIA which has been paid to the petitioners from November 1994 to

December 2006.

By an interim order dated 13th October 2008, impugned recovery order

was stayed.

It is submitted by Mr. Shamsi, learned ASGI that in view of the Section

34 of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007, only the Tribunal can hear and

adjudicate upon the subject matter of the writ petition. This position is not

disputed by the learned counsel for the petitioners.

In view of the above, it is directed that this petition be transferred for

hearing and adjudication to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Srinagar presently

stationed at Jammu. The Registry shall transfer the record of the case to the

Armed Forces Tribunal. Parties are directed to appear before the said Tribunal

on 9th August 2019.
