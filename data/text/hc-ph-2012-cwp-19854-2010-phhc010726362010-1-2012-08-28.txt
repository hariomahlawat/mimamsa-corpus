AUGUSTINE GEORGE MASIH, J.  (ORAL)

An objection has been raised by the counsel for the

respondents that the present writ petition is not maintainable as the

petitioners have an alternative remedy of approaching the Armed

Forces Tribunal as per the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2005.  She

has further stated that the claim, which has been made by the

petitioners in the present writ petition, has already been granted to

[[page 2]]
CWP No. 19854 of 2010
2

the petitioners way back as per the reply filed by the respondents.

Counsel for the petitioners states that he has no instructions in this

regard.  However, he states that liberty may be granted to the

petitioners to approach the Armed Forces Tribunal, if still any

grievance remains.

In view of the statement made by the counsel for the

parties, the present writ petition is disposed of with liberty to the

petitioners to approach the Armed Forces Tribunal, in case there is

any grievance pending with the respondents.
