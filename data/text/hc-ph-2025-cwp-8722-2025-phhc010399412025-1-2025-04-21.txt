SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA J.(Oral)

The present writ petition has been filed assailing the order dated

18.08.2023 (Annexure P-1) passed by respondent No.2-Armed Forces

Tribunal (hereinafter to be referred as ‘AFT’) whereby the AFT has directed

the petitioners to consider the case of respondent No.1 for fixation of pay

and allowances @ Rs.8,560/- per month w.e.f 01.06.2006 onwards instead

of Rs.7,630/- as per recommendations of 5th Central Pay Commission in the

light of the judgment rendered in Kultar Singh Chauhan’s case.

2.
Admittedly, the judgment passed in Kultar Singh Chauhan’s

case has attained the finality up to the Apex Court. However, learned

counsel appearing for the petitioners submits that the instant case would

have to be examined in terms of the subsequent circular issued by the

Department dated 25.03.2021.

3.
We find that the submission of the petitioners on the annual

provisions of 6th Central Pay Commission are found to be without basis.

4.
 It is an admitted position that the original applicant-respondent

had been promoted prior to 01.01.2006 and was fixed at the pay of

Rs.7,630/- with Grade Pay of Rs.2,800/- per month after the coming into

force of 6th Central Pay Commission while person junior to him who had

been directly recruited after 01.01.2006 started getting a higher pay in the

same pay band.  The recommendations of the 6th Central Pay Commission

Report specifically are provided as under:-

“(e) That in addition to that as per recommendations of

6th Central Pay Commission report “in cases where a

senior promoted to a higher rank before the 1st day of

January, 2006 draws less pay in the revised pay

structure than his junior who is promoted to the higher

rank on or after the 1st day of January, 2006, the pay in

the pay band of the senior should be stepped up to an

amount equal to the pay in the pay band as fixed for his

junior in that higher rank”

(f) That hence, as per recommendation of 6th Central

Pay Commission Pay and allowances of the petitioner

required to be fixed either @ Rs.8,560/- pm, whereas the

Basic Pay of the petitioner have been fixed @ Rs.7,630/-

per month and due to this very reasons, the petitioner is

getting less pay and allowances in comparison to the

person who was promoted to the rank of Havildar as on

01.01.2006.  As such the Basic Pay of the petitioner was

required to be fixed minimum @ Rs.8,560/- pm w.e.f.

01.01.2006, whereas his pay has been fixed by taking

into account @ Rs.7,630/- which is less than as per the

recommendation of 6th Central Pay Commission”.

5.
In view of the afore-said provisions, the original applicant was

required to fix the pay at the minimum of Rs.8,560/- w.e.f. 01.01.2006, i.e

concerned Havildar, would not deprive the right of applicant who was
