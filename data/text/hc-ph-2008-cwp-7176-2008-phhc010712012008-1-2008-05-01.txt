HEMANT GUPTA, J.

The petitioner has sought disability pension having

been discharged from the Army on 24.7.1946. In communication

Annexure P.3 dated 7.9.2007, it has been pointed out that the petitioner

was discharged due to reduction in strength and in fact, the petitioner

was in medical category shape-I and not in low medical category and

thus, the petitioner is not entitled to disability pension.

There is no document on record on the basis of which

it can be inferred that the petitioner was discharged on account of any

disability. In communication Annexure P.3, it has been categorically

pointed out that the petitioner was in Shape-I i.e. in a fit condition.

In view of the said fact, we do not find that the claim

[[page 2]]
CWP No. 7176 of 2008
(2)

of the petitioner for disability pension has any merit. Consequently, the

present writ petition is dismissed.
