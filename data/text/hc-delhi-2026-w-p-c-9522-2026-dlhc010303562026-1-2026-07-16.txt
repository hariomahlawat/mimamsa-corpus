1.
Heard.

2.
Post-Court-Martial, while serving in Indian Navy, the petitioner

suffered a punishment which was the subject matter of challenge in Writ

Petition (Civil) No.1069/1997.

3.
After the establishment of the Armed Forces Tribunal, the aforesaid

writ petition came to be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal vide T.A.

No.439 of 2009.

4.
Alleging that the final hearing in the present T.A. has been in progress

since the year 2023, but has not attended finality, learned counsel for the

petitioner has urged that the Tribunal be requested to decide the T.A. as

expeditiously as possible and, in any case, within a period of four weeks

from today.

5.
As against above, learned counsel for the respondent states that he be

granted time to take instructions in the matter, as the proceedings are

pending since long.

6.
However, in the facts and circumstances of the case, we deem it

appropriate to dispose of the present petition in the following manner

7.
We could notice from the record that on 7th September, 2022, the

Tribunal has passed an order that the matter was listed before it on 3rd

November, 2022. Subsequent thereto, we are informed by learned counsel

for the petitioner today that matter has been ‘part heard’ since the year

2023. If that be so, we deem it appropriate to direct the Tribunal to

expeditiously decide the pending proceedings of the petitioner, particularly,

T.A. No.39 of 2009, as claimed by learned counsel for the petitioner, as

expeditiously as possible and, in any case, within a period of three months

from today.

8.
We have not granted time to the respondents having regard to the fact

that the order passed is innocuous.

9.
The petition stands disposed of in above terms.
