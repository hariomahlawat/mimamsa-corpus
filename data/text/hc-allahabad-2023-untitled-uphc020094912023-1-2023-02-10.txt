Hon'ble Vivek Chaudhary,J.

Heard learned counsel for petitioner and the
learned Assistant Solicitor General of India
assisted by Sri Varun Pandey.

Petition has been filed challenging the order dated
07.12.2022 whereby his representation for being
discharged from the service on compassionate
ground is rejected.

Learned Assistant Solicitor General of India
assisted by Sri Varun Pandey, Advocate raised a
preliminary objection that the petitioner has an
statutory and alternative remedy before the
Armed Forces Tribunal under Section 3(o)(ii) & (iv)
of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

Learned counsel for the petitioner prays to
withdraw the present petition with liberty to
approach the Armed Forces Tribunal.

With the aforesaid liberty, the writ petition is
dismissed as withdrawn.

      (Vivek Chaudhary,J.)
