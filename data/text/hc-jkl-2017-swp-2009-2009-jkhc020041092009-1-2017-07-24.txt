Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the

matter is cognizable by the Armed Forces Tribunal

Bench of Jammu in view of 34 of the Armed Forces

Tribunal Act and in view of the order passed by this

Court on 18th July, 2017 in the bunch of the writ

petitions headed by SWP No. 142/2005 and for the

reasons assigned therein, this writ petition is also

transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal Bench at

Jammu.

The aforesaid prayer is not opposed by Mrs.

Sindhu Sharma, learned ASGI.

The Registry is directed transmit the record of the

instant case to the Armed Forces Tribunal Bench at

Jammu, forthwith.

Accordingly, the writ petition is disposed of

alongwith connected MP(s).
