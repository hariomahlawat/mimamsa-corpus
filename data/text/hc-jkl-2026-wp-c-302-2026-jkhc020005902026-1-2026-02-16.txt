1.
Impugned in this petition, filed by the Union of India under Article 226

of the Constitution of India, is an order and judgment dated 16.09.2025

passed by the Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench Srinagar at

Jammu [“the Tribunal”] in OA No. 206/2023 titled “Ex Hav Karnail

Singh Vs. Union of India and others”, whereby the Tribunal has held

the respondent entitled to grant of disability element for disability in

respect of “Primary Hypertension” @ 30 % rounded off to 50 % w.e.f

01.11.2022 for life.

2.
The issue raised in this petition is no longer res integra and is fully

covered by our judgment titled “Union of India Vs. Nirman Singh

Sr. No. 04
