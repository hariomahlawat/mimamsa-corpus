M.M.Kumar, J.

This is an application under Section 34 of the Armed Forces

Tribunal Act, 2007 ( in short the ‘Act’) seeking transfer of the instant writ

petition to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh, Regional Bench,

Chandigarh.

Notice of motion.

Ms. Renu Bala Sharma, Central Government Standing Counsel

who is present in the Court, accepts notice on behalf of the non-applicant-

respondent. A copy of the application has been furnished to her. Concededly,

in view of the nature of prayer made in the application, no detailed reply is

necessary, therefore, the matter is taken up for final disposal.

There is no dispute that under Section 34 of the Act, all matters

relating to the Armed Forces are now to be considered by the Armed Forces

Tribunal, Chandigarh, Regional Bench, Chandigarh.

Accordingly, application is allowed.
