1.
The present petition has been filed with a prayer to give

direction to the respondents to implement the order passed by the Armed

Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench, Chandigarh.

2.
Learned counsel for the parties agree that the issue raised in the

present writ petition has already been decided by this Court while passing

the order dated 27.08.2025 in CWP-3586-2025, titled as Malkit Singh vs

Union of India and others along with a bunch of writ petitions hence, the

present petition may kindly be disposed of in the same terms.

3.
The present writ petition is disposed of in terms of order dated

27.08.2025 passed by this Court in CWP-3586-2025, titled as Malkit Singh

vs Union of India and others.
