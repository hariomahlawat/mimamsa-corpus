The only prayer made by the petitioner in this writ petition

is that the respondents be directed to decide his statutory petition under

Section 164 of the Army Act, 1950 expeditiously.

Notice of motion.

On asking of the Court Ms. Geeta Singhwal, Central

Government Standing Counsel accepts notice on behalf of the

respondents.

After hearing learned counsel for the parties, we dispose of

this writ petition with a direction to respondent No.3 to decide the

statutory petition filed by the petitioner within a period of six months

from the date of receipt of certified copy of this order.
