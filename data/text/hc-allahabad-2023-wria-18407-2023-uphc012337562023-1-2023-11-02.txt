Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

Petitioner, who retired as Nurse, has challenged the order dated
15.10.2022, whereby, his claim for pension has been rejected.

Sri Sushil Kumar Mishra, learned Standing Counsel submits that
petitioner has an alternative remedy to approach the Armed Forces
Tribunal constituted already under the Armed Forces Tribunal Act,
2007, wherein such grievance can be raised by the petitioner.

In view of the above, I decline to entertain this petition at this
stage. Liberty rests with the petitioner to approach the Armed
Forces Tribunal, if so advised.

Subject to aforesaid liberty, this petition is consigned to records.

Order Date :- 2.11.2023
