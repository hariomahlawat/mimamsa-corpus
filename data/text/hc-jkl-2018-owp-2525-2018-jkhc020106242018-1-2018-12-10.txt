1
By way of this writ petition, the petitioner has assailed an order dated 29th

November, 2018 whereby the Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench Srinagar

at Jammu, has merely granted time to the respondents to collect the information

from the authorities concerned on the bail application filed by the applicant

therein. The matter stands listed for the first day of next sitting of the Bench i.e on

7th January, 2019.

2
In our view, the respondents would be entitled to verify the facts, which

have been stated by the applicant in the bail application.

3
 Keeping in view the right to liberty of the applicant being involved, the

matter stands posted on the first available day of the Bench.

 We are, therefore, not inclined to interfere with the impugned order.

 The writ petition stands dismissed along with connected IA.
