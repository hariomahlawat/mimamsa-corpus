This petition involves adjudication of service dispute between the

petitioner and the Union of India and the same is amenable to

jurisdiction of Armed Forces Tribunal, J&K, Jammu, in the first

Tribunal immediately.

Needless to say that the Armed Forces Tribunal, J&K, Jammu, on

record of file from this Court shall proceed in the matter only after

putting the parties to notice.
