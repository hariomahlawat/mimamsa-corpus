ORDER

Learned counsel for the appellant has submitted  that inadvertently he has

challenged judgement dated 1st of April 2024 passed by the learned Armed Forces

Tribunal, Regional Bench Srinagar at Jammu, by way of an appeal, though, no

appeal lies against the said judgment.

Learned counsel for the appellant has submitted that the appellant would

like to withdraw this appeal and file an appropriate proceeding for challenging the

impugned judgement dated 1st of April, 2024. His statement is taken on record.

In view of the above, the present appeal is dismissed as withdrawn with

liberty to the appellant to challenge the impugned judgement dated 1st of April,

2024 passed by learned Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench Srinagar at

Jammu by way of an appropriate proceeding.

Disposed of.
