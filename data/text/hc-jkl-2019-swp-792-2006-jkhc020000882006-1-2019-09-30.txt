ORDER

CM No. 5062/2019

This is an application seeking transfer of the writ petition to

Armed Forces Tribunal, Jammu Bench.

In view of the settled legal position, this Court lacks jurisdiction

to entertain the writ petition in the first instance. Accordingly, the same is

transferred to Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh (Circuit Bench Jammu).

Registry is directed to transmit the complete paper book of the writ petition

to the Registrar, Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Circuit Bench, at

Jammu. On receipt of the same, the Tribunal shall issue notice to the parties.

Application stands disposed of.
