HOBO

VIKAS BAHL, J.(ORAL)
l. The plaintiff-appellant had filed the suit for declaration to the effect

that the plaintiff is entitled for grant of disability pension. Other prayers were
also made in the suit. The said suit was dismissed by the trial Court and the
appeal filed by the plaintiff-appellant was also dismissed,

2. A perusal of the paperbook would show that no interim order has
been passed in favour of the appellant.

3. On the last date, none had appeared on behalf of the appellant and
thus, fresh notice was issued.

4. Even today, none has appeared on behalf of the appellant to inform
the Court as to whether any cause survives in the present appeal which was filed
in the year 1997 and arises from the suit of the year 1992 and accordingly, the
present appeal is disposed of at this stage.

5. Liberty is granted to the parties to revive the appeal, in case, any
cause of action survives.

6. Registry is directed to send the copy of the present order to the

parties on the addresses mentioned in the memo of parties.
