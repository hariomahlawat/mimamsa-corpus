Court No. - 36

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 12895 of 2018

Petitioner :- Ratan Kumar
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Ajay Kumar Shukla
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Mrs. Sunita Agarwal,J.

Sri Vijay Raj Pal has filed his memo of appearance on behalf of
the respondent-Union of India. The same is taken on record.

The petitioner seeks a writ of certiorari quashing the order dated
8.10.2013 passed by the respondent whereby his claim for grant
of disability pension has been rejected. The disability certificate
showing the nature of disability from which the petitioner is
suffering has not been brought on record.

In view of above, the present petition cannot be entertained.

Dismissed as such.

It is open for the petitioner to challenge the order before the
higher authority.

Order Date :- 28.5.2018
AK Pandey
