MINISTRY OF DEFENCE

NOTIFICATION

New Delhi, the 22nd March, 2017

S.R.O. 3(E).—In partial modification of the notification of the Government of Indian in the Ministry
of Defence, S.R.O. 16 (E), dated 5th November, 2009, published in the Gazettee of India, Extraordinary, Part-
II, Section 4, the Central Government hereby notifies the jurisdiction of the Bench of the Armed Forces
Tribunal at Lucknow with effect from the 9th day of May, 2016 to the territorial limits of the States of Uttar
Pradesh and Uttarakhand only.

[F. No. 5(5)/2010-D(AFTC)]

V. ANANDARAJAN, Jt. Secy.
