Court No. - 34

Case :- SPECIAL APPEAL No. - 1059 of 1998

Appellant :- Union Of India And Another
Respondent :- Sushil Kuamr
Counsel for Appellant :- Shishir Kuamr,Rakesh Sinha
Counsel for Respondent :- Rajesh Pathik,U.N.Sharma

Hon'ble Sudhir Agarwal,J.
Hon'ble Virendra Kumar Srivastava,J.

1. Heard Sri Manoj Kumar Singh, learned counsel for appellant and Sri

Rajesh Pathik and Sri Kshitij Shailendra, learned counsel for respondent.

2. Sri Manoj Kumar Singh, Advocate appearing for appellant sought time

stating that he has not prepared the matter.

3. Record shows that it is an old appeal of 1998 and on four occasions it

was dismissed for want of prosecution. Earlier on 19.09.2007, it was

dismissed for want of prosecution and restored on 24.07.2009, dismissed

on 27.11.2009 and restored on 09.04.2010; dismissed on 07.10.2010 and

restored on 01.12.2010 and lastly dismissed on 17.01.2012 and restored on

21.03.2018. It was listed on 27.05.2019 and again request for adjournment

was made by appellant and this Court passed following order:-

"1. A request for adjournment has been made by learned counsel for
appellants.

2. Being very old matter, we normally do not adjourn but in the interest of
justice we are accommodating only for today.

3. As prayed, put up on 29.5.2019"

4. Today also learned counsel for appellant has not prepared the matter and

seeks adjournment. We decline request for adjournment.

5. We ourselves have gone through judgment of learned Single Judge.

Although proceedings of court martial resulting in conviction and sentence

[[page 2]]
of rigorous imprisonment of three years and dismissal from service, were

challenged before this Court. Learned Single Judge has found that

proceedings against respondent, were not conducted in accordance with

law and punishment grounded on extra judicial confession which could not

have been basis for punishment and learned Single Judge has allowed writ

petition vide judgment dated 11.09.1998 which is a very detailed judgment

and have considered all aspects. We do not find any manifest error in

judgment of learned Single Judge.

6. Appeal lacks merit and is accordingly dismissed.

7. Let a copy of this order be sent to Law Secretary of Government of India

to see as to in what manner, pairavi of cases of Government of India has

been done by Law Officers of Central Government and repeatedly, when

old matters of 20 years or more, are not being attended properly. In the

present case, conduct of counsel for appellant as discussed above, shows

that appeal was repeatedly dismissed for want of prosecution, though

battery of lawyers are in the panel of Government of India. This is a sorry

state of affairs, and laxity shown by the learned counsel is apathetic. For

this reason, cases in which Government is party, take longer time and are

not decided expeditiously.

Order Date :- 29.5.2019
Vivek Kr.
