ORDER:

This appeal has been preferred against order of

learned Single Judge declaring the respondent to be eligible for

grant of disability pension.

There is delay of 2913 days in filing the appeal.  Only

explanation for the delay is that after seven years, the respondent

filed contempt for non-compliance, which cannot be accepted as

valid explanation for condonation even if liberal view is taken in

the matter.

We, thus, do not find any ground to condone the

delay.  Application for condonation of delay is dismissed.

Consequently, the appeal is dismissed.
