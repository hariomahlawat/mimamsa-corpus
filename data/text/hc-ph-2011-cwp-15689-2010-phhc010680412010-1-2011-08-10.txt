RANJIT  SINGH,  J.

The petitioners have approached this Court for grant of

revision of their pay scale after removing the anomalies due to

implementation of 5th Pay Commission. In the reply filed, objection is

raised about the maintainability of the writ petition before this Court in

view of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007. Apparently, the relief

claimed would fall within the jurisdiction of the Armed Forces

Tribunal. On a request made by counsel for the petitioners, let the

record of this case be transferred to Armed Forces Tribunal.

Disposed of accordingly.
