Case :- WRIT - A No. - 7764 of 2022

Petitioner :- Kaushal Kishor Yadav
Respondent :- Union Of India And 5 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Bharat Singh Pal
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Tapeshwari Prasad

Hon'ble Saurabh Shyam Shamshery,J.

Sri Tapeshwari Prasad, Sri Tapeshwari Prasad, learned counsel
for respondent Nos.5 and 6 raised preliminary objection that
this writ petition is not maintainable before this Court as the
cause of action is to be agitated before the Armed Forces
Tribunal.

At the outset, learned counsel for petitioner submits that this
petition be dismissed as withdrawn to approach the Armed
Forces Tribunal.

The writ petition is dismissed as withdrawn with the aforesaid
liberty.
