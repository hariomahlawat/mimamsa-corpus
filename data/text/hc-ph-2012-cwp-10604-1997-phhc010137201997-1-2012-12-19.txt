RANJIT SINGH, J.

The petitioner-Army Officer has approached this court

through the present writ petition for medical reimbursement. Some

amount out of that is still payable according to the petitioner. This

issue may now be within the jurisdiction of Armed Forces Tribunal

constituted under the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

Let the record of this writ petition be transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandi Mandir for further disposal.

The writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
