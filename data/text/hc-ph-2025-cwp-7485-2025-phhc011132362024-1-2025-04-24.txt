Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that in a similar case,

the matter was taken up before the Hon’ble Supreme Court, wherein it has

stayed the operation of the order. A careful look to the order passed by the

Hon’ble Supreme Court in Civil Appeal No.27246/2023, titled Union of

India and others. V. Balakrishan Mullikote, reflects that the same does not

in any manner deal with the issue, which has been decided by the AFT.

3.
In fact, learned counsel fairly admits that the directions issued

by the AFT Larger Bench at New Delhi in Shama Kaur case (supra) has been

duly implemented. The order passed by the AFT impugned before us simply

directs the petitioner to decide the case of the respondent in the same terms as

in Shama Kaur case (supra). The order passed by the AFT is simply

innocuous and does not take away any rights of the petitioner.

4.
The writ petition is wholly misconceived and is accordingly

dismissed.
