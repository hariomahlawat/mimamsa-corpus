The instant writ petition has been filed challenging the

order dated 24.12.2024 (Annexure P-22) whereby the respondents

have denied the claim of the petitioner for grant of family pension

along with arrears with respect to Shri Nand Kishore (since

deceased) who died on 10.07.2009.

Notice of motion to respondents No.1 to 3 only at this

stage.

Mr. Parvesh K. Saini, Senior Panel Counsel, enters

appearance and accepts notices on behalf of respondents No.1 to

successors in so far as the claim relates to their service matters.
