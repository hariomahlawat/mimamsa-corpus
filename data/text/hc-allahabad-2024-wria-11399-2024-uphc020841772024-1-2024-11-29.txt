2. By means of the instant petition the petitioner

has sought issuance of a direction to the Armed

Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Lucknow for

expeditious disposal of Execution Case No.68 of

2023 filed in respect of an order dated 14.07.2022,

passed in O.A. No.239 of 2022.

3. Keeping in view the fact that the petitioner's

original application was allowed in the year 2022

and the execution application is pending since

14.03.2023, this petition is disposed of by

[[page 2]]
directing the Armed Forces Tribunal, Regional

Bench, Lucknow to decide Execution Case No.68 of

2023 expeditiously without granting any

unnecessary adjournment to any of the parties.
