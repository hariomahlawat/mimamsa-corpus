“Learned counsel representing the parties on both sides

have agreed that the point in issue in this matter is squarely

covered in favour of the applicant by the judgment of

Larger Bench of Armed Forces Tribunal, Principal Bench,

New Delhi rendered in O.A. No 1238 of 2016 titled Smt

Shama Kaur Vs. Union of India and Others decided on

01.10.2019. If that be so, the respondents are directed to

consider & decide this case in the light of the ratio of the

judgment (supra) within a period of four months from the

date of receipt of certified copy of this order by learned

CGC /OIC Legal Cell and the arrears which shall remain

restricted to three years preceding the date of institution of

this application i.e. 24.01.2022 be calculated and released

to the applicant within the same time failing which together

with interest @ 8% from the date of this order till

realization.”

4.
Indisputably, the aforesaid order is a consented order.

The order passed in Shama Kaur’s case (supra), relied upon by the

learned AFT while deciding the Original Application filed by

respondent No.1, has attained finality as no writ petition or appeal has

been filed against the said order. Nothing contrary could be shown or

referred to by the learned counsel for the petitioners.

5.
In view of the above, we do not find any ground to

interfere with the order passed by the learned AFT.   Hence, the

present writ petition is dismissed.
