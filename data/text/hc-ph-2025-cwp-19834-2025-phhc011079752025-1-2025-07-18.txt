5.
A perusal of the impugned order of the Tribunal reveals that

respondent No.1 during the course of arguments had restricted his claim only

qua the conduct of the Re-survey Medical Board for assessment of his disability

if any and percentage thereof. It was in this background that the Tribunal had

disposed of the application with the direction to conduct the Re-survey Medical

Board and process the claim of the applicant for grant of disability pension in

accordance with the judgments of the Supreme Court in the case of Dharamvir

Singh Vs. Union of India (2013) 7 SCC 316 and Sukhvinder Singh Vs. Union

of India, (2014) 14 SCC 364.

6.
We do not find any merit in the argument of learned counsel for the

petitioners that the application of respondent No.1 suffers from delay and laches,

for the reason that pension is a recurring cause of action, which has been

reiterated by the Supreme Court in the case of M.L. Patil (dead) Through LRs

v. State of Goa and another, 2022 SCC Online 685. So far as the second

argument of learned counsel for the petitioners is concerned that the disability of

respondent No.1 had been assessed by the Board as less than 20% which would

make him ineligible for disability pension, the same has been rejected by the

Supreme Court in the case of Sukhvinder Singh Vs. Union of India (supra).

7.
Furthermore, the benefit of rounding off the disability to the extent

of 50% has been affirmed in the judgment of the Supreme Court in the case of

Union of India and others Vs. Ram Avtar, 2014 SCC Online SC 1761. It is

also not in dispute that the Supreme Court in its judgment passed in the case of

Bijender Singh V. U.O.I. and Ors., 2025 SCC Online SC 895 has affirmed the

judgments passed in cases of Union of India v. Ram Avtar (supra),

Dharamvir Singh Vs. Union of India (supra) and Sukhvinder Singh Vs.

Union of India, (supra) and had reiterated that wherever a member of the

Armed Forces is invalided out of service, it perforce has to be assumed that his

disability was found to be above 20% and as per the extant Rules/Regulations, a

disability leading to invaliding out of service would attract the grant of 50 %

disability pension.
