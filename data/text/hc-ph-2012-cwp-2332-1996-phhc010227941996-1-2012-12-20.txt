RANJIT SINGH, J.

The relief claimed in this writ petition is for granting one

time increase in pension. This issue would now be within the

jurisdiction of Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the Armed

Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

Let the record of this writ petition be transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandi Mandir for further disposal.

The writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
