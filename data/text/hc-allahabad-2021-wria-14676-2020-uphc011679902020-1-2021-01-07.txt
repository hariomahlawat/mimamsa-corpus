Court No. - 5

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 14676 of 2020

Petitioner :- Maheepal Singh
Respondent :- Union Of India And 5 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Balbeer Singh
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Tanisha Jahangir Monir

Hon'ble Saral Srivastava,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and learned counsel for
the respondents.

It is pointed out by Ms. Tanisha Jahangir Monir, learned
counsel for the respondents that against the order impugned
there is statutory remedy of appeal under Armed Forces
Tribunal Act, 2007.

Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that writ petition
may be dismissed as not pressed with liberty to the petitioner to
pursue the alternative remedy available in law.

Accordingly, the writ petition is dismissed as not pressed with
the aforesaid liberty.

Order Date :- 7.1.2021
