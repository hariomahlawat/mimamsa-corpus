Shekher Dhawan, J.

Present writ petition is for issuance of directions to the

respondents to  sanction disability pension to the petitioner on account of

82% permanent disability  due to the injuries inflicted upon him at the time

of his duties.

Learned counsel for the petitioner contended that for redressal

of his grievance, petitioner made representation dated 30.08.2018 (Annexure

P2), which is still pending adjudication with the respondents.

In view of above, present writ petition stands disposed of with

the directions to the respondents to look into representation dated

30.08.2018 (Annexure P2) and take the required decision thereon,

expeditiously and preferably within a period of three months from the date

of receipt of a certified copy of this order.
