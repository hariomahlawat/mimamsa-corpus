This writ petition is directed against the failure and refusal to consider

the petitioner for promotion to the higher post of Brigadier.

The
petitioner
challenged
the
action
of
the
respondents
by

approaching the Armed Forces Tribunal (Principal Bench), New Delhi. The

Armed Forces Tribunal did not pass an interim order as prayed for by the

petitioner, restraining the concerned respondents from proceeding with the

selection for promotion. The Armed Forces Tribunal, however, directed that

the selection of junior persons to the applicant, if any, by the Selection

Board held on 14-17 February, 2017 would abide by the outcome of the

proceedings before the ld. Tribunal.

The petitioner is sufficiently protected. The promotion will abide by

the result of the proceedings before the Tribunal. Our interference is not

[[page 2]]
called for. Ld. Tribunal will decide the application upon affidavits.

This writ petition is thus disposed of.
