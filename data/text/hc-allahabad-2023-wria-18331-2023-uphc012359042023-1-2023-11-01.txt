Court No. - 34

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 18331 of 2023
Petitioner :- Ravindra Nath Pandey
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Balram Jee Verma,B.S. Pandey
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

1. Heard learned counsel for petitioner and Sri K.J. Shukla, learned
Advocate holding brief of Sri N.K. Chaterji, learned counsel for
the respondents.

2. A preliminary objection has been raised by the counsel for the
respondent regarding maintainability of the writ petition as the
petitioner being an ex-army personnel, the relief claimed is
cognizable by Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the Armed
Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

3. Meeting the preliminary objection, learned counsel appearing
for the petitioner submits that earlier a writ petition being Writ-A
No.5566 of 1993 was sent to the Armed Forces Tribunal for
adjudication but the Armed Forces Tribunal transmitted the record
back to the High Court for want of jurisdiction and it is in this
circumstance that the Writ Petition (A) No.5566 of 1993 came to
be disposed of finally on 23.05.2023. He does not dispute that the
benefits as were directed to be conferred upon vide order dated
23.05.2023 have been conferred upon him and now there is no
grievance as to the controversy involved in Writ-A No.5566 of
1993. He submits that now the petitioner claims further
promotional pay scale.

4. In the considered view of the Court, since the controversy is not
arising out of judgment of this Court passed by this Court in Writ-
A No.5566 of 1993 and a fresh cause of action is there for getting
promotional pay scale, the preliminary objection is liable to be
upheld. The petitioner is, therefore, remitted to avail the remedy
available to him before the Armed Forces Tribunal.

5. Subject to the aforesaid liberty, petition is consigned to records.
