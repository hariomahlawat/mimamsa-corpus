1.
Vide this petition, the petitioner seeks initiation of contempt proceedings

against the Respondent-authorities, for willful/deliberate disobedience of the

order and judgment dated November 25, 2013, passed in SWP No. 882/2008

(Ex. Nk, Sudershan Kumar vs. Union of India and ors.).

2.
 A bare reading of the aforementioned order reveals that the petition was

disposed of with a direction to the respondent-Authorities to consider the case of

the petitioner for grant of disability pension within a specified time period .

3.
Learned Senior counsel at the very outset has shared with the Court a

copy of communication dated July 14, 2017 issued by the Col Dir, AG/PS-

4(Pension/Legal) for ADG PS vide which the petitioner has been granted

disability pension @ 20% with effect from 01 March 1998 for life.  A copy of

the communication is taken on record and marked as ‘X’.

4.
That being so, nothing substantive survives in the contempt petition and

the same is, accordingly, disposed of as having been rendered infructuous.
