Issue notice.  Mr. Prashant Sivarajan, Advocate appearing on

behalf of the respondents accepts notice.

The application seeks termination of the proceedings in this writ

petition.  The petitioner further seeks liberty in terms of Para 39 of the

Supreme Court’s Judgment in Union of India Vs. Major General

Shrikant Sharma with CA No. 7400/2013 (reported as 2015 (3)

SCALE 546).  The Supreme Court had held that High Court cannot

exercise its jurisdiction against the orders of the Armed Forces

Tribunal and that an appropriate remedy would be to approach the

Supreme Court after obtaining permission. This was so observed in

Section 30-31- as observed in para 39 of Shrikant Sharma’s Case

(supra).  In view of this development, the proceedings in this writ

petition are hereby terminated.  It is open to the writ petitioner to seek

appropriate remedies in para 39 of Shrikant Sharma’s Case (supra).

The writ petition is disposed of in above terms.
