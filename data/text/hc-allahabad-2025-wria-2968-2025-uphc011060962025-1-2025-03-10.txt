Hon'ble Jayant Banerji,J.

1.  A preliminary objection has been raised by the learned counsel
for the respondent regarding maintainability of this petition before
this Court on the ground that the jurisdiction lies before the Armed
Forces Tribunal under the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

2.  Learned counsel for the petitioner has not been able to dispute
the aforesaid objection.

3.  Accordingly, this petition is dismissed leaving it open to the
petitioner to approach the Tribunal, in case so advised.
