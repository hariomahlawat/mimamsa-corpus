25.02.2020
1. This petition was entertained at a time when the Chairperson of the

Armed Forces Tribunal („AFT‟) had not been appointed to replace the

earlier incumbent. Since then, a Chairman of the AFT has been appointed

and all matters are now being entertained in the AFT in accordance with

law.

2. The Court therefore permits the present Petitioner to now approach the

AFT, not later than four weeks from today, by filing an appropriate

application in accordance with law, and if necessary, to also invoke Rule 6

of the Armed Forces Tribunal (Procedure) Rules, since the cause of action

arose in Jabalpur, where, even as on date, there is no functioning Bench of

the AFT.

[[page 2]]
3. It is clarified that it will be open to parties to rely on the pleadings in the

present petition before the AFT. The limited interim order already passed by

this Court in the present petition is directed to continue till such time the

AFT passes appropriate orders.

4. The petition and the pending application are disposed of in the above

terms.

S. MURALIDHAR, J.
