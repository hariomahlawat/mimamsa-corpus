Petitioner had been working in Indian Army (Territorial Army). He seeks a

direction in the name of respondents not to deduct any amount from the family

pension of petitioner and enhance his family pension in view of increasing

prices of daily commodities.

Section 34 of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act provides for transfer of

pending cases, which includes suits, or other proceeding pending before any

court including a High Court or other authority immediately before the date of

establishment of the Tribunal under the Act, being a suit or proceeding the

cause of action whereon it is based, is such that it would have been within the

jurisdiction of the Tribunal, if it had arisen after such establishment within the

jurisdiction of such Tribunal, stand transferred on that date to such Tribunal.

Subsection (2) of Section 34 of the Act provides that where any suit or other

[[page 2]]
proceeding stands transferred from any Court including a High Court or other

authority to the Tribunal under Subsection (1) of Section 34 of the Act, the

Court or other authority shall, as soon as may be, after such transfer, forward

the records of such suit, or other proceeding to the Tribunal; and the Tribunal

may, on receipt of such records, proceed to deal with such suit, or other

proceeding, so far as may be, in the same manner as in the case of an

application made under Subsection (2) of Section 14 from the stage which was

reached before such transfer or from any earlier stage or de novo as the Tribunal

may deem fit.

Instant writ petition is covered under Section 34 of the Act and in view of

mandate contained therein, is required to be transferred to the Tribunal.

Accordingly, it is directed that instant writ petition be transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal Bench at Jammu after retaining xerox copy thereof.
