HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD

WRIT - A No. - 16629 of 2026

Court No. - 33

HON'BLE MANISH KUMAR NIGAM, J.

1. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, learned Standing Counsel for

State-respondents and perused the record.

2. The present petition has been filed challenging the order dated 05.08.2026

passed by respondent no. 4, situated at Jammu & Kashmir.

3. Learned Central Government Counsel has raised a preliminary objection

that this Court has no territorial jurisdiction as the authorities are at Jammu

and Kashmir. He further submitted that the petitioner can avail remedy

before the Armed Forces Tribunal.

4. Since the petitioner has an alternative remedy, I am not inclined to

entertain the instant petition.

5. Accordingly, the petition is dismissed with liberty to the petitioner to

avail the remedy as available to him under law.
