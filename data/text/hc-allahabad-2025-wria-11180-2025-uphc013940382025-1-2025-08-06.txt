Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

1. Heard learned counsel for the parties.

2. Petitioner who was working as Sepoy in the 24,
Rashtriya Rifles is aggrieved by the order of
dismissal dated 17.08.2020.

3. Learned counsel for the respondent Sri Pramod
Kumar Singh, states that the petitioner has
alternate efficacious remedy to approach the
Armed Forces Tribunal.

4. Learned counsel for the petitioner could not
dispute the above submission.

5. In view of the above, I decline to grant
indulgence in the matter.

6. Liberty rest with the petitioner to apply before
the Armed Force Tribunal, if so advised.

7. Subject to aforesaid liberty, this petition is
consigned to records.
