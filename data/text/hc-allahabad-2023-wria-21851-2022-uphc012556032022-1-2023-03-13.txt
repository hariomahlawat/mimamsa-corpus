Court No. - 37

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 21851 of 2022

Petitioner :- Ahire Nana
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Rajendra Kumar Pandey
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Narendra Singh

Hon'ble Vikram D. Chauhan,J.

At this stage, learned counsel for the petitioner submits that he
may be permitted to withdraw the present writ petition with liberty
to approach the Armed Forces Tribunal.

Sri Narendra Singh, learned counsel appearing on behalf of Union
of India has no objection and he also agrees that the Armed Forces
Tribunal has the jurisdiction in the matter.

Considering the prayer, the writ petition is permitted to be
withdrawn with liberty to the petitioner to approach the Armed
Forces Tribunal in accordance with law.

Order Date :- 13.3.2023
D. Tamang

DHIRENDRA TAMANG
