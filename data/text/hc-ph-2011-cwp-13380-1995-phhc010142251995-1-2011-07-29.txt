The prayer made in the application is for transferring the

case to the Armed Forces Tribunal at Chandi Mandir.

In view of the legal provisions contained in the Armed

Forces Tribunal Act, 2007, the present case is mandatorily required

to be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the

Act. The Registry is directed to transfer the records of the present

petition to the  Armed Forces Tribunal at Chandi Mandir.

Disposed of accordingly.
