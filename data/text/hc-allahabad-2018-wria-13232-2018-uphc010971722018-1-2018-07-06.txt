Case :- WRIT - A No. - 13232 of 2018

Petitioner :- Ravindra Kumar
Respondent :- Union Of India And 4 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Dr. Neel Kamal Sharma

Hon'ble Ashwani Kumar Mishra,J.

Petitioner is employed with the Indian Army.  His remedy, in
respect of grievance raised, can be examined by the
Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the Act of 2007.

The writ petition is, accordingly, dismissed with liberty
reserved to the petitioner to approach the appropriate
forum.
