Court No. - 37

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 7062 of 2018

Petitioner :- Ruchin Kumar
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Ashok Kumar
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Abhinava Upadhya,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and Shri
Chandra Prakash Yadav, learned counsel for the
respondents.

This is a writ petition for recruitment in the Army.
For any relief the petitioner has to approach
Armed Forces Tribunal. Writ petition before this
Court is not maintainable.

It is, accordingly, dismissed.

Order Date :- 28.2.2018
Sunil Kr. Gupta
