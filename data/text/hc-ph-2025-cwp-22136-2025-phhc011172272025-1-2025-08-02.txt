VINOD S. BHARDWAJ, J. (Oral)
Even though the petitioner raises a challenge to the punishment

awarded vide order dated 08.06.2020, however, it is not disputed by the

Counsel for the petitioner that he himself has pleaded in the writ petition that

a statutory appeal under Section 26 of the Army Act, 1950 has already been

filed with the Appellate Authority and that no decision has been taken

thereupon. He submits that he would be satisfied at this juncture in case the

respondents are directed to decide the pending appeal expeditiously and in a

time bound manner.

Notice of motion.

Mr. Vibhor Bansal, Senior Panel Counsel appears and accepts

notice on behalf of respondent(s)-Union of India.

Counsel for the respondent-Union of India submits that he has

no instructions with respect to pendency of the appeal before the Appellate

Authority. He, however, submits that the same may be time barred.

Be that as it may, once the submission of the appeal is being

claimed by the petitioner and the same is not being disputed at this stage, I

deem it appropriate to dispose of the present writ petition with a direction to

the respondent-GOC HQ 16 Corps to decide the pending appeal dated

05.08.2024 submitted by the petitioner, expeditiously and preferably within

a period of four months of receipt of certified copy of this order.

The writ petition is disposed of.
