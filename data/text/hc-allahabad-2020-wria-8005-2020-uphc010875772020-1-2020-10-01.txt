Court No. - 39

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 8005 of 2020

Petitioner :- Anant Singh Yadav
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Arvind Kumar
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Ashwani Kumar Mishra,J.

Petitioner is an Ex-Army Man.

Objection taken by Sri S.S. Sengar that the present writ
petition is not maintainable as remedy of the petitioner
would be to approach the Armed Forces Tribunal is found to
have substance.

Leaving it open for the petitioner to approach the
appropriate forum, this writ petition is consigned to
records.

Order Date :- 1.10.2020
Ranjeet Sahu
