AUGUSTINE GEORGE MASIH, J. (ORAL)

The claim in the present petition is for grant of disability

pension to the petitioner under the Central Civil Services (Extra-ordinary

Pension) Rules, 1939, as amended from time to time.

Counsel for the petitioner submits that as per the written

statement filed by the respondents, the claim of the petitioner is still

pending with the respondents and no decision thereon has been taken.  He

refers to para-16 of the written statement, wherein the said fact is

mentioned.  Counsel for the respondents does not dispute this fact that the

matter is still pending with the respondents.

In view of the above, the present petition is disposed of with a

direction to the respondents to consider and decide the claim of the

petitioner within  a period of two months from the date of receipt of a
