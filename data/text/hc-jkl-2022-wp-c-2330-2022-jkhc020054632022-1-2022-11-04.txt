ORDER
04.11.2022

Learned counsel for the respondents submits that the petitioner is ex-

serviceman and is seeking his re-enrolment in the army and, therefore, is

subject to the Army Act and the dispute raised herein is amenable to the

jurisdiction of Armed Forces Tribunal, Srinagar Bench at Jammu.

Having regard to the statement of Mr. Vishal Sharma, learned DSGI

and also looking to the nature of controversy involved, this petition is not

maintainable.

Accordingly, this petition is dismissed leaving it open to the petitioner

to work out his remedy before the concerned Armed Forces Tribunal in

accordance with law.
