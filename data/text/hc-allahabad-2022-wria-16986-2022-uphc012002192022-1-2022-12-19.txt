Court No. - 34

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 16986 of 2022

Petitioner :- Brijesh Singh
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- I.P. Singh
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Arun Kumar Pal

Hon'ble Pankaj Bhatia,J.

Heard the counsel for the petitioner and Sri Arun Kumar Pal,
the counsel for the respondents.

The present petition has been filed claiming that the petitioner
is entitled to the disability pension. The petitioner had earlier
approached this Court wherein directions were issued for
deciding the representation of the petitioner. The said
representation was rejected on 18/19-07-2019.

The petitioner claims that subsequent to the rejection order, a
clarification was issued by the Railway Board on 08.07.2022
and thereafter the petitioner moved a fresh application in the
light of the said clarification. The said application filed
subsequent to the issuance of the clarification is pending
consideration.

Considering the fact that the application of the petitioner dated
26.09.2022 is pending consideration, as informed by the
counsel for the petitioner, the present petition is disposed off
with direction to the respondent no.3 to decide the application
of the petitioner within a period of two months keeping in view
the Railway Board Circular, as relied upon by the petitioner.

Order Date :- 19.12.2022
