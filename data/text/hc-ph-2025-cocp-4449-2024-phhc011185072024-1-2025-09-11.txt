VIKRAM AGGARWAL, J. (ORAL)

The instant contempt petition has been instituted alleging

disobedience of order dated 09.02.2024, passed in CWP-26729-2013

(Annexure P-1), vide which the following directions were issued:

“4.
Learned counsel for the respondents-UOI does

not dispute the authenticity of Annexures P-4, P-5 and

P-6 and submits that Competent Authority would look

into the matter and find out reason of non-release of

disability pension in terms of aforesaid orders.

5. In the wake of statement of learned counsel for the

respondents-UOI, the present petition stands disposed

of with a direction to look into the matter and redress

grievance of the petitioner within 3 months from

today.”

2.
Learned counsel representing the respondent(s) submits that the

aforesaid order has since been complied with. In this regard, he refers to the

averments made in paragraphs 11, 12 & 13 of the reply filed on behalf of the

answering respondents, which read as under:
