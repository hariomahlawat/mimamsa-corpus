1.
In the present petition, the challenge is to the impugned order

dated 30.07.2024 (Annexure P-1) passed by respondent No. 2-Armed Forces

Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh (for short ‘the Tribunal’) by which,

the claim of the respondent No.1 for the grant of second benefit of service

pension by condoning the shortfall of 10 months and 13 days in service has

been allowed by the Tribunal so as to treat the same as 15 years of

qualifying service which is the period of service required for grant of service

pension, by placing reliance upon the judgment of the Full Bench in

O.A.1238 of 2016, titled as Smt Shama Kaur vs. Union of India and others,

decided on 01.10.2019.

2.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that the judgment in

Shama Kaur’s case (supra) was at a later point of time relied upon by

Hon’ble Delhi High Court while granting benefit of condonation of service

period, which order of Delhi High Court was challenged and Hon’ble

Supreme Court of India in the appeal preferred by the Union of India,

3.
We have heard learned counsel for the petitioners and have

gone through the case file with her able assistance.

4.
It may be noticed that the same question of law has come up for

consideration before this Court in CWP-14095-2025, titled as Union of

India and others vs. Ex.Naik Hardev Singh and another,  decided on

19.08.2025 wherein, the same plea was taken by the petitioners-UOI and

after considering the said plea, the same was dismissed, which fact has gone

unrebutted at the hand of the learned counsel for the petitioners.

5.
Keeping in view the same, present petition is also dismissed in

terms of the order passed by this Court in  Ex.Naik Hardev Singh (supra).
