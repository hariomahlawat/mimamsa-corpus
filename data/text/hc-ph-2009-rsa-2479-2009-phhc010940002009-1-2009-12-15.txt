SABINA, J.

Learned counsel for the appellant has stated that the

issue involved in this appeal is cognizable by the Armed Forces

Tribunal constituted under Section 4 of the Armed Forces Tribunal

Act, 2007.

Section 34 of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act 2007 reads

as under:-

“Transfer of pending cases:- (i) Every suit,

or other proceeding pending before any court

[[page 2]]
R.S.A.No.  2479 of  2009 (O&M)
2

including a High Court or other authority

immediately before the date of establishment of the

Tribunal under this Act, being a suit or proceeding

the cause of action whereon it is based, is such that

it would have been within the jurisdiction of the

Tribunal, if it had arisen after such establishment

within the jurisdiction of such Tribunal, stand

transferred on that date to such Tribunal.

(2)
Where any suit, or other proceeding stands

transferred from any Court including a High Court or

other authority to the Tribunal under Sub-section (1)

---

(a) the Court or other authority shall, as soon as

may be, after such transfer, forward the records of

such suit, or other proceeding to the Tribunal;

(b) the Tribunal may, on receipt of such records,

proceed to deal with such suit, or other

proceedings, so far as may be, in the same manner

as in the case of an application made under Sub-

Section (2) of Section 14 from the stage which was

reached before such transfer or from any earlier

stage or de move as the Tribunal may deem fit.”

Accordingly, this appeal is referred to the Armed Forces

Tribunal at Chandigarh for adjudication.

Parties are directed to appear before the Tribunal on

25.1.2010.

This appeal stands disposed of accordingly.
