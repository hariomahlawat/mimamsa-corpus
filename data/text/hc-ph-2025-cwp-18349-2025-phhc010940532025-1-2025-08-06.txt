1.
Learned counsel for the petitioner- UOI submits that as per the

directions given in the impugned order dated 09.03.2021 (Annexure P-1)

passed by respondent No. 2-Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench was

09.03.2021 (Annexure P-1),  has already been complied with by the

petitioners, no ground is made out for any interference by this Court in the

facts and circumstances of the present case.

3.
 Accordingly, the writ petition is dismissed.
