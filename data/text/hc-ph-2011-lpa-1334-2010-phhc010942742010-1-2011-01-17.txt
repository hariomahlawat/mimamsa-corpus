M.M.KUMAR, J.

1.
The instant appeal under Clause X of the Letters Patent is

directed against judgment dated 9.10.2009 passed by the learned Single

Judge.  In para 2 of the judgment, the learned Single Judge has recorded

the fact that counsel for the parties had agreed that the controversy is

squarely covered by a judgment of this Court rendered in Ex.-CFN Jai

Vir Singh v. Union of India and others (CWP No. 5831 of 1995,

decided on 1.9.2000.  Accordingly, the learned Single Judge allowed the

petition in terms of the aforesaid judgment.

2.
Having heard learned counsel for the parties, we are of the

[[page 2]]
LPA No. 1334 of 2010
-2-

considered view that no appeal would be maintainable against the

impugned order passed by the learned Single Judge.  Even otherwise,

the disability pension is payable to the writ petitioner-respondent

because the Medical Board has in categorical terms opined that the

disability pension was attributable to or aggravated by military service

as is clear from the communication dated 11.12.1997 (R-1).  The appeal

is, thus, without merit which is dismissed.
