Notice of motion.

Ms. Renu Bala Sharma, Central Govt. standing counsel, who is

present in the Court accepts notice on behalf of the Assistant Solicitor

General for respondents.  Copy furnished.

The petitioner joined the Indian Army on 31.10.1973 as Sepoy.

He remained posted at Indo-China Border, a high altitude and extremely

cold area.  It is stated that the petitioner's feet got infected and he was

permanently disabled to the extent of 20%.  He was put in Medical Category

'B' (Permanent) and was discharged from service on 16.10.1982.  The

grievance of the petitioner is that he has not been paid any disability

pension.  The petitioner has already made a representation (Annexure P-2),

which has not been considered till date.

Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the petitioner

will be satisfied, if, respondents are directed to consider his representation,

referred to above.

[[page 2]]

In view of the above, this petition is disposed of with a

direction to the respondents to consider the representation of the petitioner

within a period of three months from the date certified copy of this order is

served upon the competent authority.  Needless to say that in the event the

claim of the petitioner is to be rejected, the same shall be by a reasoned and

speaking order.
