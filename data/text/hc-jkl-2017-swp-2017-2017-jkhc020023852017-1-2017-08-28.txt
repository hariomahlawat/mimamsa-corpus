Per Alok Aradhe-J

Heard.

This writ petition has been filed against the order dated 07.04.2017 passed

by the Armed Force Tribunal Srinagar Bench at Jammu.  Learned counsel for the

petitioner submitted that in view of the decision of the Supreme Court in the case

of L. Chandra Kumar V. Union of India, AIR 1997 SC 1125, this Court has

jurisdiction to deal with the writ petition and examine the validity of the order

passed by the Armed Forces Tribunal.

We have considered the submissions made by the learned counsel for the

petitioner.  Section 30 of the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 provides for

remedy of appeal against the order passed by the Armed Force Tribunal to the

Supreme Court.  The aforesaid view has been taken by the Supreme Court in the

case of Union of India & others V. Major General Sri Kant Sharma, (2015) 6

SCC 773.  However, it is fairly pointed out by the learned Assistant Solicitor

[[page 2]]
Page 2 of 2

General of India that the aforesaid decision has been referred for consideration

before the larger Bench of the Supreme Court.

In view of the fact that the correctness of the view taken by the Supreme

Court in the case of Major General Sri Kant Sharma is pending consideration

before the larger Bench of the Supreme Court, in the fact situation of the case, the

writ petition is disposed of with the liberty to the petitioner to approach this Court

as and when the matter is decided by the larger Bench of the Supreme Court.

With the aforesaid liberty, the writ petition is disposed of.
