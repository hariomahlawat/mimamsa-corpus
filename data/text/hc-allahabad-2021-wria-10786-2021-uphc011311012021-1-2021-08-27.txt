Court No. - 2

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 10786 of 2021

Petitioner :- Colonel Anshuman Bhadauria
Respondent :- Union Of India And Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Abhinav Prasad
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Suneet Kumar,J.

Case called out.

None appears to press the petition on behalf of the petitioner.

Sri S.P. Singh, learned Additional Solicitor General of India,
assisted by Sri Abrar Ahmad, learned counsels appearing for the
respondents submits that the matter is cognizable under the
Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

In view thereof, the writ petition is dismissed as withdrawn
with liberty to the petitioner to take remedy under the
aforenoted Act as per law, if so advised.
