1.
In the present petition, the challenge is to the impugned order

dated 06.03.2025 (Annexure P-3)  passed by respondent No. 2-Armed

Forces Tribunal, Regional Bench, Chandigarh, by which, respondent No.1

has been allowed the benefit of disability pension by rounding off the

disability to 50% for life keeping in view the facts and circumstances of the

present case.

2.
Learned counsel for the petitioners places reliance upon the

report of medical examination of respondent No. 1 to hold that though the

disabilities of “(i) Diabetes Mellitus Type-II, (ii) Primary Hypertension &

(iii) Post Covid Sequeale” have been found in respondent No.1, but out of

the three, disability No.(i) Diabetes Mellitus Type-II, and (ii) Primary

Hypertension have been held to be ‘neither attributable to Military Service

nor aggravated by the Military service’ and disability No.(iii) Post Covid

Sequeale has been attributed to military service, which disability has also

5.
It is conceded fact that at the time when respondent No.1 retired

from service on 31.10.2023, he had already rendered more than 30 years of

the time when respondent No. 1 joined the armed forces, he was medically

examined and was not found suffering from any such disease, on the basis of

which, respondent No. 1 has been granted the benefit of disability pension.

The claim of respondent No.1 is covered in his favour for the grant of

disability pension as per the judgment of Hon’ble Supreme of India in

Dharamvir Singh vs. Union of India and others, (2013) 7 SCC 316. The

relevant para Nos.30, 32 and 33 of the judgment in Dharamvir Singh’s case

(supra) are as under:-

6.
Further, as per the settled principle of law settled by Hon’ble

Supreme Court of India in Union of India and others vs. Ram Avtar, 2014

SCC Online SC 1761, any officer serving in the Armed Forces, who had

undergone the medical examination at the time of his/her selection and was

found fit, subsequently upon suffering a disability, is entitled to the benefit

of disability pension by rounding off the same as the presumption would be

that the disability suffered is attributable to the Military service.  Relevant

paras of the judgment in Ram Avtar’s case (supra) are as under:-

“4.

has retired on attaining the age of superannuation or

on completion of his tenure of engagement, if found

We do not see any error in the impugned

judgment(s) and order(s) and therefore all the appeals

7.
Learned counsel for the petitioners has not been able to dispute

the said proposition of law having been settled by the Hon’ble Supreme

Court of India in Ram Avtar’s case (supra).

8.
Keeping in view the facts and circumstance of the present case

as well as the settled principle of law settled in Ram Avtar’s case (supra),

once at the time of selection, respondent No. 1 was medically examined and

was found fit in all respects and it was only during the service, respondent

No.1 was found suffering from the (i) Diabetes Mellitus Type-II, (ii)
