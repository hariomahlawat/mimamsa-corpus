L. N. MITTAL,  J.   (Oral) :

Suit filed by plaintiff-respondent regarding pension as ex-

serviceman has been decreed by the trial court.  First appeal preferred by the

defendants has been dismissed as time barred by the Lower Appellate Court,

vide judgment dated 08.05.2009, which is under challenge in the instant

revision petition preferred by defendants under Article 227 of the

Constitution of India.

The matter involved in the instant revision petition relating to

grant of pension is covered by Section 34 and Section 35 of the Armed

Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 and therefore, in view of said provisions, the

instant revision petition has to be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal,

Chandimandir Bench and is accordingly transferred to the said Tribunal for
