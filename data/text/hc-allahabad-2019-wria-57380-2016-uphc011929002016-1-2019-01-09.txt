Court No. - 6
Case :- WRIT - A No. - 57380 of 2016
Petitioner :- Mr. Mrityunjay Kumar Mishra
Respondent :- Union Of India And 6 Ors.
Counsel for Petitioner :- Rajesh Kumar Mishra
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Ankur Sharma,Jai
Shanker Audichya

Hon'ble Yashwant Varma,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Ankur Sharma,

learned counsel who has appeared for the sixth and seventh

respondents.

A preliminary objection has been raised to the maintainability

of this petition before this Court, firstly on the ground as it

relates to a service matter of an armed forces personnel and

would therefore be liable to be presented only before the

concerned Tribunal. Additionally, it is pointed out that none of

the respondents reside within the territorial jurisdiction of this

Court.

Learned counsel for the petitioner is unable to establish or

demonstrate that any part of cause-of-action arose within the

territorial jurisdiction of this Court.

Accordingly, this petition is dismissed both on the ground of

lack of territorial jurisdiction as well as the matter being liable

to be presented for adjudication before the concerned Armed

Forces Tribunal.

It is further provided that the amount of Rs.10,000/- which was

deposited with the Mediation Center of this Court pursuant to

the initial order dated 6 December 2016 shall be released in

favour of the sixth respondent forthwith.

Order Date :- 9.1.2019
Arun K. Singh
