SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA J.(Oral)

The challenge in this writ-petition is to the order dated

12.07.2023 (Annexure P-1) passed by respondent No.2-Armed Forces

Tribunal (for short ‘the AFT’) whereby the AFT has proceeded to grant the

benefit of War Injury Pension to the applicant (respondent No.1 herein) in

terms of the letter dated 31.01.2001 as he suffered Frost Bite Both Feet

while being deployed in China Operation.

2.
 As noticed earlier by us in a separate judgment, the injury

admittedly was caused during the China Operation and falls under Category

E (i) the disability occurred on 28.08.1998 and thus, would be available for

grant of coronary pension.

3.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that the same was

claimed after a long delay would be wholly without basis as the
