It, however, appears to us that Sri Chccma has attained the age of A

In the facts of the case, we feel that the ends of justice will be met

impugned order of the High Court is set aside and punishment of Sri

We only add that the charge of corruption alleged against the said
officer is quite serious which requires that a deterrent punishment should
be passed. However, in the peculiar facts of this case, we have awarded
the aforesaid lesser punishment and we may indicate that this case should
not be treated as a precedence for other cases of corruption.
