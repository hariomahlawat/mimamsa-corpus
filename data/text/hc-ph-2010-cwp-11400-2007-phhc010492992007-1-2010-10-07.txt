At the outset, learned counsel for the parties have stated that all service

matters concerning members of Armed Forces involving  pension and other

retiral benefits  pending before this Court, are required to be transferred to

the Armed Forces, Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench, Chandigarh (for brevity

“Army Tribunal), which is constituted for this purpose under Armed Forces

Tribunal Act,2007.  These writ petitions  have been filed under Article

226/227 of the Constitution challenging the order passed by the Army

Authorities, which now can be heard by the Army Tribunal.

After hearing learned counsel for the parties, we are of the view

that these matters are liable to be transferred to the Army Tribunal.

We order accordingly.

The parties through their counsel are directed to appear before

the Army Tribunal on 15.11.2010.

All these matters along with original record be sent to the Army

Tribunal by the registry expeditiously but not later than two weeks.

Writ petitions stands disposed of.

A photocopy of this order be placed on the file of each

connected cases.
