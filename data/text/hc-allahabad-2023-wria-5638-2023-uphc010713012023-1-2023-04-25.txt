Court No. - 37

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 5638 of 2023

Petitioner :- Diksha Tiwari And Another
Respondent :- Union Of India And 5 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Rajendra Kumar Ojha
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Jai Prakash Rai

Hon'ble Saral Srivastava,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioners and Sri Jai Prakash Rai,
learned counsel for the respondents.

The petitioner by means of present writ petition has claimed for a
mandamus directing the respondents no.3 and 4 to consider the
petitioners' application dated 13.01.2023 annexed as Annexure-4
to the writ petition.

Perusal of the relief claimed, indicates that it pertains to service
matter and therefore, the present dispute is cognizable by the
Armed Forces Tribunal in view of Section 14 of the Armed Forces
Tribunal Act, 2007.

Accordingly, the writ petition is dismissed on the ground of
alternative remedy.

Order Date :- 25.4.2023
