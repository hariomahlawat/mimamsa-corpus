Surya Kant, J. (Oral)

The petitioner is an Ex-Naik of the Indian Army who was

dismissed from service on September 27, 1985 soon after the Operation

Blue Star. The petitioner was also subjected to the Court Martial and was

convicted and sentenced to undergo RI for 8 years. It is averred that the

Central Government later on remitted the sentence of the convicts like the

petitioner and his RI was reduced from 8 years to four years.  The

petitioner further claims that vide a subsequent decision, the Central

Government has further decided to grant `pensionary benefits' to the Army

personnel like him who had deserted the Army due to the alleged

emotional outburst.

In this regard, the petitioner is said to have served the

respondents with a legal notice dated 21.3.2009 (Annexure P-4) but

without any response thereto.

Though there is nothing on record to substantiate the afore-

mentioned plea taken by the petitioner, however, keeping in view the

categoric averments made in this regard, I deem it appropriate to dispose

of this writ petition with a direction to respondent Nos.2 & 3 to consider

the afore-stated claim of the petitioner, if so permissible under the Rules/in

accordance with law, and communicate the decision to him preferably

within a period of four months from the date of receiving a certified copy

of this order.

Ordered accordingly.
