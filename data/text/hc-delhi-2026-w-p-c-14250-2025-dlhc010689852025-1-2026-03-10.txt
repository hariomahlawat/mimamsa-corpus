Union of India & Ors.

3.
In the communication received, it is requested that six months be

granted for deciding the O.A.

4.
The learned counsel for the petitioners in the writ petitions state that

in the proceedings before the Tribunal, the Tribunal has stayed the

disciplinary proceedings and also the subsequent court of inquiry because of

which the petitioners are unable to proceed against the respondent.

5.
In any case, it is his submission that since the Court has remanded the

matter back to the Tribunal, appropriate could have been for the Tribunal to

hear the matter on the date fixed, by this Court i.e., 13.02.2026 or on any

date to which the matter has been adjourned, and decided the same.

6.
Without going into the submissions advanced by the counsel, we

deem it appropriate to request the Tribunal to hear the counsel for the parties

and decide the OA 3664/2023 Maj. Priyanka Singh vs Union of India & Ors.

within a period of three months from today as an outer limit.

7.
This answers the communication received by the Registry of the

Court from the Joint Registrar of the Armed Forces Tribunal.
