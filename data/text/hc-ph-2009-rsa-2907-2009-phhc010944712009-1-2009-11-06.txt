M.M.S. BEDI, J. (ORAL)

For the reasons mentioned in the application, the delay in

refilling the appeal is condoned.  Application stands disposed of.

Counsel for the appellants has contended that the plaintiff-

respondent has wrongly been granted disability pension as the disease i.e.

Generalized Tonic Clonic Seizure, 345 V-64 is neither attributable nor

aggravated by the military service and further the same has been assessed to

be less than 20%.

[[page 2]]
R.S.A. No. 2907 of 2009(O&M)                                                               [2]

I have heard counsel for the appellants.   The Courts below

have given a concurrent finding of fact that the appellant had suffered the

ailment on account of stress and strain of the army service as such he is

entitled to disability pension.  No reasonable ground arises for interfering in

the finding of fact as it is apparent from the evidence produced by the

plaintiff that he suffered ailment on account of stress and strain of the

service of the army.

Dismissed.
