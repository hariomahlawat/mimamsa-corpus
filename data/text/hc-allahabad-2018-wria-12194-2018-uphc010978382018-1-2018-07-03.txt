Court No. - 58

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 12194 of 2018

Petitioner :- Smt. Guddi Devi
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Kailash Prasad Pandey
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Ashwani Kumar Mishra,J.

Dispute relating to apportionment of family pension pursuant to
death of army personnel who was the husband of petition need
not be entertained by this Court inasmuch as petitioner has
remedy of approaching the Armed Forces Tribunal. Claim of
this kind can always be raised before the Armed Forces
Tribunal in view of section 14 of the Armed Forces Tribunal
Act, 2007.

Writ petition is, accordingly, dismissed.

Order Date :- 3.7.2018
Ashok Kr.
