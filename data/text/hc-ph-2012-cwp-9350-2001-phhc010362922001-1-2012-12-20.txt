RANJIT SINGH, J.

The prayer made in this writ petition is for remustering the

petitioners from one cadre to another. As per the counsel for the

Union of India, this will relate to the terms and conditions and would

now fall within the jurisdiction of Armed Forces Tribunal constituted

under the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

Let the record of this writ petition be transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandi Mandir for further disposal.

The writ petition is accordingly disposed of.
