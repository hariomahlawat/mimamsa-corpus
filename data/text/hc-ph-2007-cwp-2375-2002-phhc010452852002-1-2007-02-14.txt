ASHUTOSH MOHUNTA, J. (ORAL)

The petitioner has prayed for quashing the order Annexure P-2

& P-9 by which his disability pension was discontinued by the respondents.

The petitioner has also prayed that in case the disability pension cannot be

granted to the petitioner then in the alternative he be granted service

element of the disability pension in terms of Regulation 186(2) of the

Pension Regulations for the Army.

A perusal of the order shows that the petitioner who was

enrolled in the Navy on 17.3.1966, as a Boy was granted temporary

disability pension as he suffered from a disease which was diagnosed as

AMBOEBIC LIVER ABSCECC (ICD-572).  In view of the disability of the

petitioner he was invalidated out of the military service in the year 1977, as

his disability was assessed at 40%.  The disability of the petitioner was

reassessed and the same was discontinued vide order Annexure P-2 as his

disability was reduced to less than 20%.  The petitioner was informed that in

[[page 2]]

case he is aggrieved by the order (Annexure P-2) he can file an appeal

within 6 months.  However, the petitioner chose not to file any appeal.

Thus, the orders passed on behalf of Commodore, Bureau of Sailors

(Annexure P-2) is upheld and the petitioner is not entitled to any disability

pension.

As far as the service element is concerned, the same has been

released w.e.f. 25.11.1985 and on 1.11.1996, the petitioner has been granted

service element of Rs.1275/- per month for life.  The service element

granted to the petitioner from 1985 to 1996 is as follows:-

1. Service element of Rs.40/- P.M. w.e.f. 25.11.85 to 31.12.85.

2. Servicce Element Rs.375/- pM w.e.f. 01.01.86 to 31.12.95.

3. Service Element Rs.1275/- PM w.e.f. 01.01.96 for life.

Counsel for the petitioner has prayed that the petitioner be

awarded interest on the delayed payment of service element.

A perusal of the facts narrated above shows that the disability

pension of the petitioner was discontinued w.e.f. 25.11.1985.  From that

very date the petitioner has been granted service element.

Counsel for the petitioner has, however, argued that although

the service element has been awarded to the petitioner w.e.f. 25.11.1985,

but the service element was given to the petitioner only on 12.9.2003 and

therefore, the petitioner is entitled to interest on the delayed payment.

Counsel for the petitioner has also relied on Annexure P-5, which is a copy

of representation claiming the service element.

From a perusal of the facts, it is clear that the petitioner was

[[page 3]]

entitled to be granted service element w.e.f. 25.11.1985, but the same was

granted to him only on 12.9.2003.  No reasons have been given in the

written statement filed by the respondents as to why the service element

could not be given to the petitioner earlier and further no reasons have been

given explaining the delay in grant of the service element.  Hence, the

petitioner would be entitled to interest.

In view of the above, this writ petition is disposed of with a

direction that the petitioner is entitled to interest at the rate of 6% on the

delayed payment of service element w.e.f. 38 months prior to the filing of

the writ petition i.e., from January, 1999 till the date of payment.
