Court No. - 34

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 10689 of 2021

Petitioner :- Sep/Na Krishna Nand No. 15444533h
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Sanjai Singh
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Akhilesh Kumar
Srivastava

Hon'ble Yashwant Varma,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and Sri Akhilesh

Kumar Srivastava, who appeared for the Union respondents.

The respondents take the objection that the petition would not

be maintainable before this Court and would have to be

instituted before the appropriate Armed Forces Tribunal. In

view of the aforesaid, learned counsel for the petitioner prays

for permission to have the instant writ petition being withdrawn

subject to rights being reserved to approach the appropriate

Tribunal.

Petition is consequently dismissed with liberty to the petitioner

to approach the concerned Tribunal, if so chosen and advised.

Order Date :- 1.9.2021
Arun K. Singh
