JAGMOHAN BANSAL, J. (Oral)

1.
The petitioner through instant petition under Articles 226/227 of

Constitution of India is seeking directions to the respondents to grant disability

pension under Category ‘E’ instead of Category ‘D’ of CCS (Extraordinary

Pension) Rules, 1939.

2.
The petitioner was enrolled on 07.07.1991 in CRPF as Constable.  He

on 25.02.1995 along with other members of Armed Forces was travelling by train

No.5908 Brahmaputra Mail.  A bomb blasted in the said train while it was passing

through Assam bordering Nagaland.  In the said incident, 06 officials were killed

and 05 including the petitioner were seriously injured.  The petitioner continued to

perform his duties till 2021.  The respondent vide order dated 29.01.2022

invalidated him because he was assessed 95% disability.  He was granted

extraordinary pension under Category ‘D’.

3.
Mr. Ujwal Anand, Advocate submits that case of the petitioner falls

under Category ‘E’ as contemplated by office memorandum dated 03.02.2000
