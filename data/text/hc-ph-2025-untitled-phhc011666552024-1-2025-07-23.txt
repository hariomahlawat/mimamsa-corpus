SHEEL NAGU, CJ. (Oral)

 1.
The present Public Interest Litigation has been filed by a

public-spirited person raising the grievance of shortage of Members in

the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh.

2.
During the pendency of this petition, certain appointments

have been made and present situation reveals that the Armed Forces

Tribunal, Chandigarh is short of only one Judicial Member.

3.
Mr.Satya Pal Jain, Additional Solicitor General of India

informs that on 03.06.2025, an advertisement has been issued for inviting

applications from suitable persons for filling up various vacancies of

Armed  Forces  Tribunals  at  different  locations,  including  Chandigarh.

4.
In view of the aforesaid, this Court has no manner of doubt

office of Judicial Member, Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh.

5.
With the hope and expectation that the selection process will

be brought to a logical end at the earliest, this Court disposes of this

petition with liberty to the petitioner to re-visit the Court, if the grievance

remains unredressed.
