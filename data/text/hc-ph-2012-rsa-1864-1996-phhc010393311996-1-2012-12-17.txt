RANJIT SINGH, J.

In view of the provisions made in Sections 33 and 34 of

the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007, this Regular Second Appeal

may also have to be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal

constituted under the Act, ofcourse subject to the condition that the

matter involved is within the jurisdiction of the Tribunal. Since the

relief of promotion is claimed in this Regular Second Appeal, the

matter would be within the jurisdiction of the Tribunal.

Let the record of this Regular Second Appeal be

transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandi Mandir for further

disposal.

The Regular Second Appeal is accordingly disposed of.
