Heard Shri Rajesh Datt Singh, learned counsel for the petitioner
and Shri S.B. Pandey, Senior Advocate & D.S.G.I. assisted by
Shri Varun Pandey, learned counsel for the respondents.

The entire cause of action in the present case appears to have
arisen at Delhi. Even the proceedings before the Armed Forces
Tribunal have culminated at Delhi. We would have entertained
the petition before this Court under Article 226 of the
Constitution of India but for the lack of territorial jurisdiction,
this Court is not inclined to entertain the petition.

The writ petition is accordingly rejected with liberty open to the
petitioner to assail the impugned orders before the Forum
having territorial jurisdiction.

The writ petition is accordingly consigned to records.
