Court No. - 16

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 8079 of 2018

Petitioner :- Jafarul Nisha
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Aktar Ahmed Siddiqui
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Ashwani Kumar Mishra,J.

Petitioner's claim to be the second wife of the deceased, who is
claiming family pension, on the ground that earlier wife has
already died. An objection has been taken by the respondents to
the maintainability of the writ petition, on the ground that
petitioner has remedy of approaching the Armed Forces
Tribunal, in view of the provisions of the Armed Forces
Tribunal Act, 2007.

Section 14 read with Section 2 of Sub-section (2) of the Act of
2007 makes it clear that not only the serving officers but even
retired person including dependents, heirs and successors shall
also be governed by the Act of 2007, in so far as it relates to
their service matter. In such circumstances, petitioner would
have the remedy of approaching the Tribunal.

Leaving it open for the petitioner to avail the remedy, as may be
available in law, this writ petition is consigned to records.
