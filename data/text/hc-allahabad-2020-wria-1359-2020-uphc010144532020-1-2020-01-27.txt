Court No. - 30

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 1359 of 2020

Petitioner :- Deepak Yadav
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Jeet Bahadur Gautam
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I., Sri Vivek Kumar Singh

Hon'ble Pankaj Bhatia,J.

Heard Sri Jeet Bahadur Gautam, counsel for the

petitioner and Vivek Kumar Singh, counsel for the

Union of India.

The counsel for the petitioner seeks to withdraw the

present writ petition to approach the Armed Forces

Tribunal constituted under the Armed Forces Tribunal

Act, 2007.

In view of the said statement, the present petition is

dismissed as withdrawn with liberty to the petitioner

to approach appropriate forum as may be advised.

Order Date :- 27.1.2020
