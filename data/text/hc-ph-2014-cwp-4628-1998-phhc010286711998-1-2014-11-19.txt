Applicant-respondent seeks transfer of the writ petition to

the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh.

Notice of the application to the counsel opposite.

At this stage, Mr. Anupam Bansal, Advocate appearing on

behalf of Mr. Ashu M. Punchi, Advocate accepts notice on behalf of

the petitioner and fairly states that he has got no objection in case

instant application is allowed and the writ petition is transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh.

In view of the abovesaid respective stands taken by learned

counsel for the parties, instant application is allowed and the writ

petition along with record, if any, is ordered to be transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal.
