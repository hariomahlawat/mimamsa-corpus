The petitioner herein has challenged the order dated 04.03.2017

discharging him from service from Territorial Army.

Learned counsel for the parties are agreed and submitted that the

jurisdiction to deal with the matter has now been conferred on the Armed Forces

Tribunal. The matter deserves to be transferred to AFT, Regional Bench

Srinagar at Jammu (for short “the Tribunal”).

Let the record of the case be transferred to the Tribunal after retaining a

copy of the paper book with the Registry of this Court for record.

The parties are directed to appear before the Tribunal on 07th January,

2019 for further proceedings.
