SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA J.(Oral)

The petitioners have preferred this writ-petition assailing the

order dated 04.12.2019 (Annexure P-1) passed by the Armed Forces

Tribunal and no action was taken against the said order for years together.

2.
The writ-petition has been filed in 2025 solely based on the

judgment passed by Hon’ble the Supreme Court in Union of India vs.

Parashotam Das, 2023 INSC 265. Prior to that right of appeal was available

if there was any legal point involved but the petitioners chose not to file an

appeal before the Supreme Court.

3.
In view thereto, the writ petition is dismissed on the ground of

gross latches.
