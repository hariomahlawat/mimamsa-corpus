Court No. - 36

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 10243 of 2018

Petitioner :- Veer Singh
Respondent :- Union Of India And 3 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Girish Kumar Singh
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Mrs. Sunita Agarwal,J.

Shri Tapeshwari Prasad, Advocate, has put an
appearance on behalf of the respondents.

A preliminary objection has been raised with regard to
maintainability of the writ petition with the assertion that
the petitioner has to approach the Armed Forces Tribunal
constituted under the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

The petitioner has retired from the post of Sepoy in the
Indian Army.

It would be appropriate that for the grievances raised
herein, the petitioner shall approach the Armed Forces
Tribunal.

The writ petition is, accordingly, dismissed.

Order Date :- 19.4.2018
