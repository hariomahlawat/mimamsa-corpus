SANJEEV PRAKASH SHARMA J.(Oral)

The challenge in this writ-petition is to the order dated

07.03.2019 (Annexure P-1) passed by respondent No.2-Armed Force

Tribunal (for short ‘the AFT’) whereby the AFT granted War Injury

Pension to the applicant (respondent No.1 herein) for life w.e.f. 01.09.2006

in terms of the letter dated 30.10.1987 which allows grant of War Injury

Pension to cases where the degree of disability is assessed as below 20% at

the time of invalidment or at any subsequent reassessment stage.

2.
We notice that the AFT has also taken into consideration the

letter dated 31.01.2001 which allows grant of War Injury Pension. It is an

admitted position that respondent No.1 was invalided out of service on

account of the war injury which occurred during the ‘Operation Rakshak’

resulting in permanent disability.

3.
Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that the said injury

caused during the ‘Opeartion Rakshak’ was less than 20%.

4.
However, in view of the Ministry of Defence letter dated

31.01.2001, we are of the firm view that if there is no embargo in taking

War Injury Pension even if the degree of disability is less than 20%, the

said War Injury Pension element has to be added. Accordingly, the instant

writ-petition is without any force. The order  passed by the AFT-respondent

No.2 on 07.03.2019 (Annexure P-1) is upheld.  We notice that this writ-

petition has been filed in the year 2025 challenging the order passed on

07.03.2019 (Annexure P-1) without any clarification. No reasons are

coming forward for inordinate delay and the writ-petition has to be

dismissed on this count too.

5.
Accordingly, the present writ-petition is dismissed.
