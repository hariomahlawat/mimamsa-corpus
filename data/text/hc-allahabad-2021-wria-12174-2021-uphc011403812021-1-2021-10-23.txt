Although counsel for the respondent Sri Shashi Shankar
Tripathi is present, but none appears on behalf of the
petitioner, even in the revised list.

Maintainability of this petition is also questioned on the
ground that this Court has no territorial jurisdiction and
that petitioner otherwise has the remedy of approaching
Armed Forces Tribunal.

Writ petition, accordingly, is dismissed for want of
prosecution.
