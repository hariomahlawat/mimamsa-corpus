For Respondent(s) :         Mrs. Sindhu Sharma, ASGI.
Order:

Mrs. Sharma, learned ASGI states that in view of

the decision of the Hon’ble Division Bench dated

18.07.2017 in SWP No.142/2005 and other connected

writ petitions, which in turn is based on the decision of

the Hon’ble Supreme Court in Union of India & ors. v.

Major General Shri Kant Sharma & anr. (2015) 6 SCC

773, grievance which is the subject-matter of the instant

writ petition, is maintainable only before the Armed

Forces Tribunal Bench at Jammu at the first instance,

therefore, the instant writ petition has to be transferred

to the Armed Forces Tribunal Bench at Jammu for

adjudication in accordance with law.

Learned counsel for the petitioner prays that in the

circumstances, the writ petition be transferred to the

Armed Forces Tribunal Bench at Jammu for adjudication

in accordance with law.

[[page 2]]
Statement of learned counsel for the parties is taken

on record. Writ petition is transferred to the Armed

Forces Tribunal Bench at Jammu for adjudication in

accordance with law after issuing notice to the parties.

Registry to take steps to transmit the records to the

Armed Forces Tribunal Bench at Jammu.

 (B. S. Walia)
