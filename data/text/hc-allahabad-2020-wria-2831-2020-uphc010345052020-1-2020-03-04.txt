Court No. - 55

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 2831 of 2020

Petitioner :- Santosh Kumar Yadav
Respondent :- Union Of India And 2 Others
Counsel for Petitioner :- Tarkeshwar Yadav
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.,Sandeep Kumar
Srivastava

Hon'ble Saral Srivastava,J.

Heard learned counsel for the petitioner and Sri Sandeep Kumar
Srivastava, learned counsel for the respondent nos.1 to 3.

The petitioner alleges that earlier he has served in foreign
country at UNMSN/IMTRAT and as such he cannot be posted
second time in foreign country. The aforesaid contention has
been raised on the basis of corps order 14/2014 as amended
from time to time and in the aforesaid backdrop, he has prayed
mainly for the following relief:-

"i. issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of mandamus commanding
the respondents to send the petitioner in foreign mission as per his posting
in Israel as per letter dated 29.03.2019 and 01.04.2019 by considering
and deciding the application/representation of the petitioner dated
06.11.2019 within stipulated time frame fixed by this Hon'ble Court,
during the pendency of the present writ petition before this Hon'ble Court,
otherwise the petitioner shall suffer irreparable loss and injury."

A preliminary objection has been raised by learned counsel for
the respondents that matter pertains to the service conditions of
petitioner in armed forces and the relief prayed for by the
petitioner can be granted by Army Tribunal constituted under
the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 (hereinafter referred to as
'Act, 2007'). Consequently, the writ petition is not maintainable
by this Court.

As it is evident from the relief sought by the petitioner, the
matter relates to the terms and conditions of service of the
petitioner in armed forces, therefore, petitioner has alternative
remedy of filing application before the Army Tribunal
constituted under the Act, 2007.

Consequently, the writ petition is dismissed on the ground of
alternative remedy.

Order Date :- 4.3.2020
