Rakesh Kumar Garg,J.(Oral)

This appeal pertains to the dispute between the parties regarding

entitlement of the pensionary benefits of the appellant who was in the service of

the Indian Army.

After coming into force the Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007, the

jurisdiction of this Court is barred and the matter can only be heard by the Armed

Forces Tribunal as considered under the aforesaid Act.

In this view of the after, this application is allowed. Let this appeal

be transferred to the Armed Forces Tribunal, Chandigarh Bench at

Chandimandir.

Disposed of.

December 1, 2010
