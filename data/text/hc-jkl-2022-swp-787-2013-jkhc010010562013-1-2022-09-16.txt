ORDER
16.09.2022
The petitioner is seeking release of pensionary benefits stated to have

accrued to her on account of the death of her husband who was the member

of Indian Armed Force.

 The subject matter of the instant petition does not fall within the writ

jurisdiction of this Court but is amenable to the jurisdiction of Armed Forces

Tribunal.

In view of above, the matter is transferred to the Armed Forces

Tribunal. Registry to send the instant petition to Armed Forces Tribunal,

Jammu Bench forthwith after retaining the soft copies for record purposes.
