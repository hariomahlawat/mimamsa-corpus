Court No. - 34

Case :- WRIT - A No. - 15577 of 2022

Petitioner :- Padala B. Shankara
Respondent :- Union Of India And Another
Counsel for Petitioner :- Sanjay Kumar Jaiswal
Counsel for Respondent :- A.S.G.I.

Hon'ble Pankaj Bhatia,J.

Present petition has been filed stating that the petitioner is
entitled to disability pension which has not been provided.

For the remedies sought in the writ petition, the petitioner has
alternative efficacious remedy of approaching Armed Forces
Tribunal.

In view of the above, the petition is disposed off permitting the
petitioner to approach Armed Forces Tribunal for the relief.
