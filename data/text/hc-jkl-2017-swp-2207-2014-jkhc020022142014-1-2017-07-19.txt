Order:
Learned counsel for the parties are in agreement that

the writ petition is not maintainable in view of decision of

Hon’ble Supreme Court  reported as (2015) 6 SCC 773

titled Union of India vs. Major General Srikant Sharma,

therefore, the petitioner is liable to be relegated to avail

his remedy before the learned Armed Forces Tribunal

Jammu.

Statement of learned counsel for the parties is taken

or record. In view thereof, instant writ petition is

transferred to learned Armed Forces Tribunal Jammu.

Registry is directed to take appropriate steps for

transmitting the file to the learned Armed Forces

Tribunal, Jammu.

 (B. S. Walia)
