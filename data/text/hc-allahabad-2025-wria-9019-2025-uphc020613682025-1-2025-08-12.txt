1. Heard learned counsel for petitioner and Sri S.B.Pandey,
learned Senior Advocate/Deputy Solicitor General of India
assisted by Sri Varun Pandey, learned counsel for respondents.

2. After hearing the parties and perusing the record, what comes
out is that on 09.07.2025,  MA No.485 of 2025 for earlier
hearing was listed before the Armed Forces Tribunal at
Lucknow, however, instead of confining itself to the said
application while passing the order the interim relief prayer has
been rejected. His submission is that the vires of provision
under which maintenance has been granted to the petitioner's
wife is under challenge before the Principal Bench of the
Armed Forces Tribunal at New Delhi and therefore, there was
no occasion to reject the interim relief application during
pendency of the matter before the Principal Bench.

3. On being confronted, counsel for opposite parties fairly
submitted that Tribunal should have confined itself to the listing
application.  The matter is already listed on 14.08.2025.  We
therefore, quash the impugned order dated 09.07.2025 but only
in so far as the interim relief prayer has been rejected, meaning
thereby interim relief application or prayer shall stand restored
and would be considered at the appropriate stage as per law.

4. The petition is accordingly allowed.
