Hon'ble Ajit Kumar,J.

1. Heard learned counsel for the parties.

2. Petitioner before this Court prays for a writ of mandamus
to the respondent no.3 for the service benefits to be
conferred upon him which were not paid to him when he was
posted in Army.

3. A preliminary objection has been raised by Sri Ajit Kumar
Srivastava, learned counsel for the respondents that the
petitioner being an employee of armed forces has remedy to
approach the Armed Forces Tribunal constituted under the
Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.

4. Learned counsel for the petitioner could not dispute the
fact that his status was of an employee of the Indian Army
and the service benefits that he prays for relates to the
service period rendered in Army.

5. Accordingly, in my considered view, the matter is
cognizable by Armed Forces Tribunal at first instance. In the
circumstances, this Court declines to grant indulgence in the
matter.

6. Liberty rests with the petitioner to apply before the Armed
Forces Tribunal, if so advised.

7. With the aforesaid liberty, petition is consigned to
records.
